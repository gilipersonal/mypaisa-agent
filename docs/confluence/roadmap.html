🧮 How the dates are worked out

  • Build days. A story's estimate covers every door it touches. Claude builds, Arun reviews and tests.
  • Buffer. Each phase adds 20% for review rounds, the demo and fixes.
  • Side by side. The web app and the iOS app are built in parallel, so a phase's working days are its build days with the buffer, divided by 1.5.
  • Days off. English bank holidays and a Christmas break each year.
  • Order. Build order from Mon 2 Nov 2026. Phase 15 waits for 8 weeks of shadow stances after phase 10; phase 16 is built when a new kind of holding is added.
  • Your tasks (label manual) run beside the build and are due before their phase starts.
