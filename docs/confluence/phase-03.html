What grows at a known rate, in both countries, and every figure in pounds, rupees, dollars or euros.

  • Deposits, savers and policies (MA-93): fixed and recurring deposits, UK savers, PPF, EPF, NPS and policies such as LIC, in the UK and India, registered through the chat from the kind specs of phase 2 (MA-197); an FD's on_maturity (renew, renew with interest, pay out); an RD with an annual step-up.
  • Wealth maths for what accrues (MA-94, ARCHITECTURE.md §6): deposit and policy accrual, the maturity timeline and the daily NetWorth series, to the penny. The loan schedules follow in phase 4 (MA-204) and XIRR in phase 5 (MA-212).
  • The old tracker's maths as golden fixtures (MA-105): the old wealth tracker holds no real data (its families are invented and nothing persists), but it has well-tested maths and a complete set of screens. Its xirr.ts, amortisation.ts, forecast.ts, budget.ts and currency.ts cases are ported as golden fixtures with a parity check, and its screens are listed for the Overview (MA-95). The family's real data arrives through the chat.
  • ECB rates, rupees included (MA-44): ECB FX rates and market calendars loaded daily, INR in the FX chain; the ECB-rate estimate for an amount in the wrong currency (MA-156) joins here.
  • Four currencies (MA-213): net worth and every figure in GBP, INR, USD or EUR (your ask of 29 Oct 2026, MA-275), a switch per member, with the ECB rate and its date shown; rupees in lakh and crore; the four views agree to the penny at the day's rate. Records are in the UK and India today; one in the US or the euro area is added as data (its kinds and priors) when it is needed.
  • What a maturity does (MA-202): a check-in before and on the maturity date asks whether the deposit was renewed, paid out or moved; a renewal, with or without the interest, starts a successor record linked to the old one; nothing is deleted.

Tests: accrual for every deposit and policy kind against fixtures to the penny, the old tracker's cases included; the maturity timeline; the daily net worth; the ECB loader on recorded responses, rupees included; the GBP, INR, USD and EUR views of the same household agreeing to the penny at the day's rate, with lakh and crore grouping for rupees; a maturity's check-in before and on the date; a renewal starting its successor, and nothing deleted; Playwright, XCUITest and local Maestro p03 flows (320 pt Display Zoom rupee hero fit, the currency switch across GBP, INR, USD and EUR with lakh/crore grouping and privacy masks, MA-273) for the currency switch.
