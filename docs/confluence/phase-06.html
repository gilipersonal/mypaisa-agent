What comes in and what goes out, each month, per member and for the household, filled from statements and one short check-in.

  • Budget (MA-96): each member's income and its history (past years' figures, so growth can be measured), from the chat or payslips, stored as income commitments; a monthly plan per category, the categories data you change by chat, EMIs and card payments from their commitments; the Budget screen with plan against actual per category, income, EMIs, the surplus and the savings rate, per member and for the household, in GBP or INR. Income commitments accept dated future changes and each category carries its inflation key, so phase 7's plan items can change them (MA-261); every amount is take-home, with no tax maths. The Projection that used to sit with the budget is phase 7.
  • Statements (MA-218): a bank or card statement sent in the chat (PDF, CSV or XLSX; UK and Indian banks and cards), kept in S3 for good; each line categorised, the merchant memory first and Haiku for the unknowns; card payments, moves between your own accounts and EMIs matched to their commitments are recognised as transfers, so nothing counts twice; a payment on a statement answers its check-in; one review card by category, uncertain lines in amber, and one Confirm. A statement is routine, so it takes no second check. The parsers are built from your anonymised samples.
  • The month closed on the 1st (MA-220): last month's plan against actual; the agent asks only for what no statement covered (cash, other banks) and closes the month with one Confirm.

Tests: statement parsers on anonymised fixtures from UK and Indian banks and cards, in each format; every line categorised; transfers recognised, so nothing counts twice; a payment on a statement answering its check-in; one Confirm writing the month, and the file kept; the month closed on the 1st, asking only for what was not covered; plan against actual per category, per member and for the household, in GBP and INR; salary growth measured from its history.

Exit: you send a bank statement in the chat; it comes back categorised, transfers recognised, uncertain lines in amber, for one Confirm; the Budget screen shows plan against actual.

Cost: Haiku only for the merchants the memory does not know, inside the ceiling; nothing more in AWS.
