MyPaisa becomes a small team of agents with narrow jobs, every behaviour a setting the admin controls, every change outside a setting's band the admin's approval in the chat or the Admin center, every prediction graded daily, and repairs made where they are safe, as ARCHITECTURE.md §20. Epic MA-127. For one household and its one admin, the owner.

  • E2, the admin desk (MA-130). Agents' requests and questions, and the admin's instructions, in the admin's chat and the Admin center (Approvals, Settings, Agents, Incidents); approval with the second check; a daily digest, incidents at once; designed first.
  • E3, the data steward (MA-131). yfinance, Twelve Data's free tier and free websites first, with failover, gap repair and disagreement checks; EODHD only on an approved request showing the evidence and the price.
  • E4, the prediction store and daily grading (MA-132). Every prediction kept as made in S3 and graded after each close; ScoreDaily in DynamoDB.
  • E5, daily calibration (MA-133). Maps refitted daily on a rolling window, at most two points a day per bucket, used only when better, on 10 days' probation with automatic rollback.
  • E6, challengers (MA-134). Retrains, parameters, rule thresholds, new patterns and features, and heavier model families (sequence models over the candles and indicators, a wider ensemble) tried over ten years and in shadow; promoted only on evidence and the admin's approval; the old version restored if the new one slips. This is how the Forecaster grows over the years (your ask of 29 Oct 2026: "self learning and self growing over time").
  • E7, the ops warden (MA-135). The daily run as one Step Functions state machine with retries; safe repairs on its own; an incident for the rest; heartbeats from every agent; dead-lettered work replayed on its own once its cause is healthy, at most three times, then an incident (MA-146).
  • E7, one trace (MA-235). The daily run is one trace from the tick to the brief's push: Step Functions and the event bus carry it, each agent is a service on the map, a replay links to the run it repeats, an incident names its trace, and every heartbeat is a metric (ARCHITECTURE.md §10.8).
  • E8, budgets for the team (MA-136). Each agent's share of the plan's $200 Claude credit, metered by agent; paid feeds recorded as subscriptions against the AWS and others cap of MA-137.
  • Each agent guarded and tested on its own (MA-256, ARCHITECTURE.md §23.5). Every agent of the team has its own manifest entry and safety suite; a ChangeRequest that changes a prompt, a rule, a threshold or a model carries the affected suites' results from dev, and a failing safety case blocks its approval.

Order. E1, the settings store, is built in phase 9 with E9 and E10, the household's learning (your pick, 29 Oct 2026); E2 can come forward once the Admin center and the second check exist (phase 8, MA-102); E3 after phase 10; E4 and E5 once stances are graded (phase 13); E6 with the Forecaster (phase 17); E7 and E8 alongside.

Tests: a member cannot reach the desk; approval without the second check is refused; an agent cannot move a setting outside its band; grading never runs before a horizon; calibration's daily step limit and rollback; a challenger that does not beat the current version is never proposed; injected failures at each pipeline step recover idempotently or open an incident, and the trace shows the step and the incident names it (MA-235); metering by agent; local Maestro p18 Admin desk Approvals, Settings, Agents and Incidents flows behind BiometricKit_Sim Face ID (MA-273).
