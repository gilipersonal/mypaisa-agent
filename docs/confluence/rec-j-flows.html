🧭 MyPaisa Agent Journey

26 Oct 2026 · MA-141, India added in MA-155, the iOS app in MA-161, re-phased in MA-186, the whole wealth's flows in MA-226, a wealth tracker only from MA-307 (2 Nov 2026) · design only, nothing is built yet

How a household uses MyPaisa, an agentic wealth tracker driven by the chat, one flow at a time: what the member does in the MyPaisa app on an iPhone or an iPad, or in the web app in a browser, the path it takes through the app, AWS and Claude, what the member sees, and what keeps it safe. The app is a chat where the household keeps every record, with the dashboard of its wealth one tap away; the web app has the same chat and dashboard, and the admins' Admin center, in a browser on a laptop or a phone. Admins add, correct and delete through the chat (correct also in place); every other member views. A team of agents keeps the records, the budget and the wealth forecast up to date from the household's actual numbers: XIRR and CAGR from the dated ledger, the Projection and the Budget forecast. The UK holdings come first; the household's Indian funds, shares and ETFs join them in rupees, with pounds beside every figure. Each flow names the Jira stories that build it.

Every figure, rate and fund name on this page is illustrative. "You" are Arun, the household's owner and an admin; Asha is the second member. 📏 Four rules every flow keeps

From ARCHITECTURE.md §1. Each path below shows where they bite.

Rule 1

Claude never produces a number

Every figure comes from code with tests. Claude chooses what to say and how to say it.

Rule 2

The app cannot trade

No broker login, no orders. MyPaisa never says buy, add, hold, pause, trim or sell: it keeps the record and forecasts the household's wealth from its actual numbers, and nothing predicts a market.

Rule 3

Summary first, detail on tap

Every message and every card has a plain top and a technical underside. Nothing technical is shown unasked.

Rule 4

Admins write, through the chat

Only admins write. There can be several; the owner is always one, and grants or takes back the role in the chat, with Confirm, the second check and an audit row (MA-308). Adding and deleting happen only in the chat: a card, a Confirm tap, the second check for a delete, an audit row. A delete marks the record deleted: hidden from every view, restorable through the chat, its events, audit rows and files kept for good (MA-309). An existing figure that its kind marks editable is corrected in the chat, or in place on a screen, at either door. Every other member views only, their own preferences aside. All of it goes through one write service, which writes the audit row. The Admin center, in the app or the web app, acts on admin settings and operations, never on a record, each behind the second check and an audit row. Arun, 2 Nov 2026 (MA-307): "I can add anything but only via chat. Editing of existing assets is an option. Even Deletion will have to go throw AI chat. Only admins can do all these."

📖 How to read a flow

Each path is a numbered sequence across five lanes. An arrow is a hand-off from one lane to another; a box is work done inside one lane. Safeguards are underlined in coral. On a narrow screen the same steps stack, each tagged with its lanes.

Member

You (an admin) or Asha (a member who views), in the MyPaisa app on an iPhone or an iPad (or a foldable, folded or open), or in the web app in a browser on a laptop.

App and web app

MyPaisa, one app for iPhone and iPad on iOS 26, a React Native shell with a native SwiftUI core: the chat with its cards, composer and shortcut chips; Home, Activity and You; the Admin center for the admins; the Keychain, Face ID, push and the share sheet. It talks to AWS over HTTPS (api.mypaisapp.com), and replies stream to it over Server-Sent Events from the same host. Beside it, the web app at dashboard.mypaisapp.com: a SvelteKit 3 progressive web app with the same chat, the dashboard and the Admin center.

AWS

API Gateway (the api's REST API, with the SSE stream as its streaming route on the same host), the api λ, the SQS FIFO updates queue, the agent λ, the SQS work queue and worker λ, the SQS outbox and sender λ, DynamoDB, S3, Cognito, EventBridge Scheduler, Step Functions (phase 13), SNS, CloudWatch.

Claude

The Anthropic API: Sonnet 5.5 and Haiku 5.5, and Opus 5.5 only for an admin's plan with me session in a loop of its own; server-side web search and fetch, Message Batches.

External data

Free sources first, for prices only, to value what the household holds: yfinance, Twelve Data's free tier, ECB FX, issuer pages, FT. For India: AMFI's NAV file from phase 5; from phase 14, mfapi.in, yfinance .NS and .BO, NSE and BSE bhavcopy, the RBI/FBIL rate. Apple and Google, when they vouch for a sign-in; Apple's push service (APNs), when the app is closed.

🛡️ The safeguards, in one place

They recur across the flows, so here is what each one means.

The policy gate and the evals

Code checks every tool call against the agent's manifest, the member's role and the run's caps, and refuses when in doubt; text from a file or a page is data, never an instruction. Every change is tested before it merges, every live run is checked by code, and the judges are calibrated by the admin (MyPaisa Guardrails and Evals, ARCHITECTURE §21).

Confirm before any write

Anything new is shown as a card first and saved only after an admin taps Confirm on it; a delete also needs the second check; a check-in's Paid is the Confirm of a card that shows exactly what it saves. A correction in place shows its before and after, then Save. A change across several records is one plan with one Confirm.

The audit row

An AuditEvent in the same DynamoDB transaction as the change: who, when, through what, before and after, which run. Hash-chained, never deleted.

Privacy mode

The eye on Home and in You, or /privacy, hides pounds and rupees in every card, screen, chart axis and notification; percentages stay, and the app switcher shows only the mark (MA-171). On the currency check's cards (MA-156, ARCHITECTURE §4.7) it shows no amount and no rate, only words, currencies and dates.

The currency check

Every holding keeps the one currency it is bought and valued in. An amount typed in another is never saved as it is: MyPaisa says why and asks for the amount that arrived (MA-156).

Signed in, in the app or the browser

Sign in with Apple or Google (or, in the browser, a passkey), exchanged for Cognito tokens: in the app they live in the Keychain, this device only; in the web app behind an HttpOnly cookie page scripts never see. Five-minute access tokens; token, status and session epoch checked on every call and on the stream.

The second check

In the app, a fresh Sign in with Apple or a passkey under Face ID (a Google-only member signs in with Google again); in the web app, a passkey or a fresh sign-in. Bound to one action and verified by the server: an import, a rollback, a delete, granting or taking back the admin role, leaving the household, and every admin action, from the Admin center or the admin's chat, approving a new kind among them (MA-197). A local Face ID alone never authorises anything.

The spend ladder

The month's Claude spend is read before every call; at 70, 85, 95 and 100% of the ceiling MyPaisa steps down. Alerts are never dropped.

Retries and dead-letter queues

Every hand-off goes through SQS: retried with backoff, idempotent, and kept in a dead-letter queue with an alarm and a replay when it keeps failing. The app keeps what is typed offline and catches up when it opens.

23The Projection, and a what-if
The Projection is the household's wealth forecast: its net worth over the years, worked out from its actual numbers (XIRR and CAGR from the dated ledger, contractual rates, salaries, debts, commitments and plans), never from a market prediction. Code computes every figure, month by month to the horizon; the agent explains. The Budget forecast, its third tab, is graded each month against what happened (E9, MA-276).
