23 · The Projection, and a what-if

The Projection is the household's wealth forecast: its net worth over the years, worked out from its actual numbers (XIRR and CAGR from the dated ledger, contractual rates, salaries, debts, commitments and plans), never from a market prediction. Code computes every figure, month by month to the horizon; the agent explains. The Budget forecast, its third tab, is graded each month against what happened (E9, MA-276). The screen has three tabs, Net worth, Plans and Budget forecast, and opens at 20 years, with 5, 10, 20 and 30 to choose (MA-261); this flow is drawn at 20 years, to 2046. From £457,951 on Mon 26 Oct the middle path reaches £1,508,000 by 2036 and £3,697,000 by 2046, and every assumption shows its source. 🛡️ Safeguards on this path

Code computes, the agent explains

Every figure on the screen and in the chat is the engine's. Claude proposes assumptions with their reasons, fills a what-if's override, explains the curve and raises the flags code computes.

A what-if is never saved

A typed override the validator checks against your words or a named preset; the result is an unsaved scenario card.

Every assumption has a source

A contract; a prior, a versioned setting with its source; or history shrunk toward a prior, weighted by how many years there are.

Admins only

Plan items and saved assumptions. Members view the Projection and ask what-ifs, but save nothing. A plan change takes no second check.

Reproducible

Each run keeps its inputs hash (records, priors and every active plan item's version), seed and engine version; a record closing or being corrected, a statement confirmed, a month closed, a plan item changed or an assumption changing runs it again.

Constraints kept

Debts amortise to their real end dates and free their payments; a locked pension cannot cover a shortfall before it unlocks.
