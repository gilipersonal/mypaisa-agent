🛰️ From a tap at either door to Claude and back, on AWS, in one trace.

The household's wealth tracker end to end, driven by the chat: the iOS app and the web app, the AWS services between them, the concierge's tool loop on the Anthropic API, the six agents that keep the records, the budget and the wealth forecast current, and which Claude model each piece uses. Admins add, correct and delete through the chat; every other member views. The forecast comes from the household's actual numbers: XIRR and CAGR from the dated ledger, the Projection and the Budget forecast. Pick a journey below and press play to watch it move through the system.

From architecture-v4.html (MA-244), live at MyPaisa Architecture v4, where each journey plays on demand and any part can be inspected.

🗺️ Every hop a message makes, on the services that carry it

Nothing waits on anything directly: what you send, what MyPaisa sends back and every background job travel through SQS, which retries them and keeps what still fails. The agent Lambda is the only one that talks in the chat, and the sender the only one that delivers to the doors. Tap any part to inspect it.
