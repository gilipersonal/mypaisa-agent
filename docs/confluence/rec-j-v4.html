🛰️ From a tap at either door to Claude and back, on AWS, in one trace.

One household's wealth tracker, end to end and driven by the chat: the iOS app and the web app, the AWS services between them, the concierge's tool loop on the Anthropic API, the six agents that keep the records, the budget and the wealth forecast current, and which of three Claude models each piece uses.

From architecture-v4.html (MA-244), live at MyPaisa Architecture v4, where each journey plays on demand and any part can be inspected.

🗺️ Every hop a message makes, on the services that carry it

Nothing waits on anything directly: what you send, what MyPaisa sends back and every background job travel through SQS, which retries them and keeps what still fails. The agent Lambda is the only one that talks in the chat, and the sender the only one that delivers to the doors. Pick a journey and press Play, step through it with the arrows, or tap any part to inspect it and see which journeys use it.

🌙 The nightly run: 8 steps

Prices in, only to value holdings; code works out net worth, XIRR and CAGR, runs the Projection again, and the insight job looks for what matters.

#StepWhat happensOn the wireModel
1Prices firstEach finished job queues the next. The prices job runs each instrument's chain of sources.work: prices → views → projection → insights
2Free data firstThe first source that answers with fresh data wins, inside the free tiers' limits; stale is the last entry everywhere, labelled. A price only values a holding; nothing predicts a market.lse_etf: yfinance → twelvedata → issuer_page → stale
3Kept as it was knownRaw answers for replay, curated prices with the date each became known, in Parquet.s3://mypaisa-prod-lake/raw/ · curated/
4Code works out the figuresEach holding's value, net worth, the attribution of what moved, and XIRR and CAGR from the dated ledger: all code, with golden tests. Claude is not called.NetWorth · Attribution · xirr(ledger) · cagr(first, last, years)
5The Projection runs againThe deterministic path month by month, then about 5,000 joint, fat-tailed paths of returns, inflation and GBP/INR. Each month's surplus stays as cash. The run stores its seed, the goals' chances and the sensitivity table.ProjectionRun(inputs_hash, seed) · goal_odds · sensitivity[] · short_odds
6The insight job looksDetectors in code: budget drift, idle cash, a goal off its glide path, a maturity or a rate change coming, a salary not credited, a currency move.
