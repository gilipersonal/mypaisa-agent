03 · Adding a holding from a factsheet or a screenshot

Attach the fund's factsheet PDF from Files, then a screenshot of its value in the Monzo app from Photos (or take either with the camera or a document scan). Claude reads them; code checks them; nothing is saved until the member taps Confirm. Anything Claude is unsure of comes back in amber.

Trigger a PDF or a picture attached in the chat, or /add Phase 2 (reading a file) Phase 5 (a fund)

👀 What the member sees

  • Attach. The composer's + opens a glass menu: the camera, a document scan, Photos and Files.
  • The factsheet card. Two fields in amber, asked about. Identifiers shortened here.
  • The screenshot. Read, matched to the holding, saved only after Confirm.

🛤️ Safeguards on this path

Confirm before any write

A holding is never saved without the Confirm tap. The same path handles an ETF, a share or cash.

Amber when unsure

Any field Claude is unsure of is shown in amber and asked about.

A source for every figure

Extracted figures carry the line they came from. Claude reads; it does not invent a number.

Straight to the private bucket

The app uploads through a presigned link that works once, for one file, for a few minutes; nothing passes through the chat's queue but its name.

Every file kept, and yours to see

Each file you send is kept for good in a private, encrypted bucket and opens from the Audit screen through a five-minute link, for you and the owner only (MA-148, MA-149).

A document's words are data

Text inside a PDF is untrusted and never followed as an instruction; no tool can trade or delete.

Retries

The document read is a job on the work queue: five tries with backoff, then a dead-letter queue and an alarm.

04 · Logging money in plain words

Type it the way you would say it, in any currency. When the sentence has everything and its currency matches the holding's, it goes straight to a Confirm card; with no currency named, the holding's own is assumed and said on the card. When something is missing, MyPaisa asks for that one thing on a card with buttons, edited in place so the chat stays tidy. Rupees typed for a pound holding take the currency check of flow 15.

Trigger "I put £200 into Balanced today", "1.5 lakh into my Nifty 50 index fund", "I want to log a transaction", or the Log money chip Phase 5 (pound and rupee holdings)

🛤️ The path

#LanesWhat happens
1Member to AppYou type I put £200 into Balanced today
2App to AWSPOST /v1/chat/messages, then your lane on the SQS FIFO updates queue: your messages one at a time, in order; Asha's run in parallel
3AWSagent λ reads this cycle's spend meter (one item), then builds the current conversation
4AWS to ClaudeThe conversation and the strict tools to Sonnet; tool_choice stays auto
5Claude to AWSClaude calls propose_change: a buy of £200 in Balanced, today. Every detail is there, so no questions
6AWSA parser in code reads the amount: £ $ € ₹, the codes, the words (pounds, rupees, Rs) and lakh and crore. £200 against Balanced's own currency, pounds from its factsheet's Confirm card: they match
7AWSCode works out the units from the NAV: about 10.8 at 18.57. A PendingAction row holds the proposal for up to a day
8AWS to AppThe Confirm card, as the agent's JSON with every figure from code, through the outbox and sender λ
9Member to AppYou tap Confirm
10App to AWSThe tap goes to /v1/chat/actions, acknowledged at once and queued in your lane like a message
11AWSTransaction and the Position's units saved with an audit row, in one transaction; the card edits in place
✎NoteMissing a detail? ask_user ends the turn with one question on a card with its buttons. The next tap resumes the flow from the PendingAction; whatever was already said is skipped; a day of silence clears it. An amount in another currency than the holding's is not saved as typed: it takes the currency check of flow 15.

👀 What the member sees

  • One sentence. Everything was said, so it goes straight to Confirm.
  • The guided flow. Shown as a sequence here; in the app each answer edits the same card, and Pick a date opens the system date picker. It ends on the same Confirm card.
  • No currency named. The holding's own is assumed and said on the card, never guessed.

🛤️ Safeguards on this path

Confirm before any write

propose_change saves nothing; only the Confirm tap does.

Numbers from code

Claude fills the fields when the wording is free; a parser in code reads the amount, and the units and the NAV come from code. The figure is always the member's own, echoed back.

The holding's own currency

Every position keeps the currency from the Confirm card that added it. An amount in another currency goes to the check of flow 15, never straight in.

Nothing asked twice

It never asks for what it can look up, and skips every question the sentence already answered.

A day, then gone

A PendingAction expires after 24 hours; an expired Confirm is refused.

Confirm needs a connection

A Confirm tapped offline is refused on the device, so nothing is saved by surprise when the signal returns (MA-173).

The audit row

Who, when, "via typed" or "via button", before and after, and the run that made it.
