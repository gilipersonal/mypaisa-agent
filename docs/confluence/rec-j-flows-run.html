10 · The Admin center, admins only

The admins' room, in the app (in You on the iPhone, in the iPad's sidebar) and in the web app on a laptop, for the admins alone: the owner, who always is one, and any member the owner has made one in the chat (MA-308). People first: each member's role and signed-in devices, with the actions that end them. Spend: the Claude ceilings, the AWS cap and where alerts go. Later, the agents' requests for approval. Every action needs the second check: in the app a fresh Sign in with Apple or a passkey under Face ID (Google again for a Google-only member), in the browser a passkey or a fresh sign-in.

11 · When something fails

Every hand-off goes through an SQS queue. A bad minute at Apple's push service, at the Anthropic API or at a data source costs a delay, never a message or a day's work. What keeps failing waits in a dead-letter queue until someone, or later the ops warden, replays it. A lost signal on the member's side costs nothing either: the app keeps what is typed and catches up when it is back.

🛤️ The path

#LanesWhat happens
1AWS to External dataworker λ's ingest job for one holding calls a source, which times out
2AWSThe message returns to the SQS work queue after a growing wait: five tries with backoff. Meanwhile the chain falls through, and the holding is valued at its last price, labelled stale
3AWSAfter the fifth try it moves to the dead-letter queue: kept 14 days, with a copy in S3 under raw/dlq/ for good
4AWSA CloudWatch alarm fires the moment the dead-letter queue holds anything; the alert router sends failed work
5AWS to AppYour chat and the Admin center's Alerts page, in the app and the web app, show it with Replay; a push if the app is closed
6Member (owner) to AWSYou tap Replay once the cause is fixed, and pass the second check
7AWSThe messages move back to their queue. Handlers are idempotent, keyed by the client id, the message id, or the job id and date, so nothing runs twice
✎NoteReplay, by phase. Phase 1: scripts/redrive.sh <queue>. Phase 8: the Replay button on the alert and in the Admin center. Phase 13: the ops warden replays on its own once the cause is healthy, at most three times per message with growing gaps; failing the same way again opens an incident.
8AWSA member's message that fails three times within about a minute goes to the updates dead-letter queue, and the member is told it was kept
9AWS to AppReplayed after more than an hour, a message is answered still want this? rather than acted on
10Member to AWSA Confirm tap whose PendingAction has expired is refused, so an old tap never saves a change by surprise
11Member to AppOn a train with no signal, you type a message: it waits on your iPhone with its client id and a clock mark. A Confirm tapped now is refused on the device
12App to AWSBack online, the app sends it once and asks for every turn after the last one it has; the chat API's dedupe makes a resend harmless

👀 What the members see

  • The owner's failed-work alert. Phase 8 adds the Replay button; replaying is an owner action, behind the second check.
  • The member's side. Kept, asked again when old, and an expired Confirm refused.
  • Offline. A typed message waits with its clock mark and goes once back online; Confirm needs a connection; figures carry their time.

🛡️ Safeguards on this path

Backoff, not hammering

Growing waits; the work queue's concurrency keeps the free sources inside their limits; the agent's reserved concurrency keeps Claude spend in bounds.

Idempotent

Every handler records what it has done, so a retry, a replay or a resend from the app never does anything twice.

Fail fast on the hopeless

A malformed message, an unknown member or an instrument with no source goes straight to the dead-letter queue with its reason.

Never silent

An alarm the moment a dead-letter queue holds anything; alerts and check-ins are always attempted.

Old taps are not trusted

Over an hour old: "still want this?". An expired Confirm: refused. A Confirm without a connection: refused on the device.

Nothing depends on the stream

On opening, the app asks for every turn after the last one it has; what was typed offline is kept with its client id and sent once.

Bounded self-repair

The ops warden replays at most three times, then raises an incident with the errors attached.

🧩 Built by

Each member's messages travel through ordered queues, in and out, are retried with backoff and are never lost
Background jobs run from a work queue with retries, and every failure lands in a dead-letter queue that alerts and can be replayed
Alerts reach the app and the web app, push and email; "failed work" carries a Replay button
Offline, the app shows the last figures with their time, keeps what is typed and sends it once back online
Replies, cards and record changes stream to every open door over SSE, in order and never twice, and an app catches up when it opens
E7: an ops warden repairs what it safely can and opens an incident when it cannot (phase 13)
A dead man's switch and the error alarms tell the owner, in the app and by push, when a job did not run
Every Lambda run leaves a RunRecord, logs, traces and an error alarm

12 · The Claude spend ladder in practice

The agents' allowance is the Max 20x plan's $200 monthly credit: $160 for prod and $40 for dev, each a ceiling the owner moves. As the month's spend climbs, MyPaisa steps down to cheaper ways of working before it stops. Alerts always go out.

Alerts are never dropped at any step: a template needs no model. Raising the ceiling moves MyPaisa back up the ladder at the next call.

✎NoteBehind the meter. Each Anthropic workspace, prod and dev, has its spend limit set by hand to the hard limit ($160 prod, $40 dev), which stops the key outright if the meter is ever wrong. Behind that, the credit itself: no payment method, auto-reload off. The meter is reconciled weekly against Anthropic's own usage figures.

📌 The guards under the ladder

Per member

No member spends more than 60% of the ceiling; past it, that member's chat runs at the 95% step for the rest of the cycle.

Per day

No day spends more than three times the cycle's daily average, about $16 at a $160 ceiling; past it, the rest of the day runs at the 95% step.

Per run

At most eight model calls in one run; a loop that reaches it stops and says so.

Per agent (phase 13)

Each agent has its share of the ceiling, the concierge 55%; past it, that agent drops to its 95% step and the reserve covers the chat first.

  • 95%. A check-in as its plain template: the same figures from code, the words from the template.
  • 100%. One line; everything with a button keeps working. The renewal date is illustrative.

13 · The first of the month: a contribution's check-in

The old first-of-the-month question is now a check-in (ARCHITECTURE §4.9). A usual contribution is a commitment, made when its record is confirmed, so on its day code puts a check-in card in the chat of its member, here you, an admin: did your usual £200 go into Balanced? The card shows exactly what Went through saves, so that tap is the Confirm, with Undo. The same morning the budget's own check-in closes last month (flow 22), and several due together fold into one card. The buy updates the holding's XIRR and the Projection runs again.

👀 What the members see

  • Today. A check-in card in 1b's style: Went through is the Confirm, saved with Undo.

🛡️ Safeguards on this path

Asked by code, never assumed

The daily job is code with no LLM, and a check-in is made once. Nothing is logged until an admin answers; an unanswered one shows as overdue, in amber.

The tap is the Confirm

Went through saves exactly what the card shows, through the same write service as every change, with its audit row; Undo writes a reversal.

No amount on the lock screen

The push asks about "your usual contribution"; the £200 is shown only inside the app, unless the member chose amounts in notifications.

Admins answer

Answering a check-in writes a record, so only an admin answers it; every other member views. The app cannot trade, and the card never says buy or sell: it records what happened.

"I put £200 into Balanced" becomes a transaction after Confirm
