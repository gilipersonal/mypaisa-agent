You can ask anything about what the household owns, owes, earns and spends, and where it is heading, and the answer comes from the records (MA-62).

  • The tool loop with strict tools, tool_choice always auto; the composer's shortcut chips and each screen's Ask.
  • Answers from the household's own tools: query_ledger and query_ledger_sql (MA-104) for the records and their history, XIRR and CAGR from the dated ledger, the budget month, the Budget forecast and the Projection; a scenario card for a what-if, unsaved, from phase 7's engine. Nothing predicts a market, and nothing says buy, hold or sell.
  • Anthropic's server-side web_search and web_fetch, results treated as untrusted text under phase 1's rule, given only to a call that holds no household figure, and a conversation that has read them narrowed by the policy gate (MA-68, MA-247); /why.
  • Natural-language preferences with Confirm.

Tests: each card against fixtures; scripted conversations on recorded responses; every figure in an answer traced to a tool result; a question asking for a buy or sell call answered with the household's numbers and a plain refusal of the call; search results containing instructions are not acted on; local Maestro p11 question, answer and Scenario card flows across iPhone and iPad (MA-273).
