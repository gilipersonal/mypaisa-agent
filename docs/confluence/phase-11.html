You can ask anything about what the household owns, owes, earns and spends, and where it is heading, and the answer comes from the records (MA-62).

  • The tool loop with strict tools, tool_choice always auto; the composer's shortcut chips and each screen's Ask.
  • Answers from the household's own tools: query_ledger and query_ledger_sql (MA-104) for the records and their history, XIRR and CAGR from the dated ledger, the budget month, the Budget forecast and the Projection; a scenario card for a what-if, unsaved, from phase 7's engine. Nothing predicts a market, and nothing says buy, hold or sell.
  • Anthropic's server-side web_search and web_fetch, only to look up a holding's public details when it is added (its ISIN, its listing, its fund house's page; ARCHITECTURE.md §7), never to explain a move in value: results treated as untrusted text under phase 1's rule, given only to a call that holds no household figure, and a conversation that has read them narrowed by the policy gate (MA-68, MA-247). A move in value is explained from code's own figures: the FX change and the holding's currency split.
  • Natural-language preferences with Confirm.
  • Reasoning-quality evals and leaner tools (MA-321, ARCHITECTURE.md §21.3, §21.4, §7.1): capability cases graded against the engine (goal solving, attribution, three-way comparisons, ambiguous plans), judges for faithful causes, asking only what is missing and fact not advice, rule 2's safety cases on both sides, and trajectory metrics in every run; the five overlapping reads merged into get_wealth, with get_projection and get_returns added.
  • Plan with me (MA-322, ARCHITECTURE.md §7.2): an admin starts a deeper planning session (retirement, education, a house) on Opus 5.5 in its own loop, with the read and planning tools only; it ends in one plan card whose every change takes the admin's Confirm; at most 5% of the ceiling, waiting from the 85% step; designed first.

Tests: each card against fixtures; scripted conversations on recorded responses; every figure in an answer traced to a tool result; a question asking for a buy or sell call answered with the household's numbers and a plain refusal of the call; search results containing instructions are not acted on; local Maestro p11 question, answer and Scenario card flows across iPhone and iPad (MA-273).

Exit: ten real questions about the household's wealth answered from its records, each figure with its working under Technical.

Cost: about £10 a month cumulative, plan with me on Opus about £3.85 of it on the standard setup and never more than its 5% of the ceiling (ARCHITECTURE.md §7.2, §14).
