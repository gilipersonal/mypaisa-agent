Shares, funds, bonds and gold in the UK and India, each inside its account, priced every day from free sources, with its real return.

  • The PriceSource contract (MA-41) of ARCHITECTURE.md §5.1: one plugin per feed (yfinance, twelvedata, eodhd, issuer_page, ft, proxy, screenshot, stale, and amfi for India), a registry that imports only the plugins in use, per-plugin identifiers on the instrument, and chains per kind set in infra/<env>/terraform.tfvars (and a .env for local). yfinance is the default chain head in every environment; the EODHD plugin ships with the others, switched on by putting it first in the chain and adding its key.
  • Daily prices, free (MA-207): each holding priced every day from yfinance for listed lines and AMFI's NAV file for Indian mutual funds; London lines quoted in pence normalised to pounds; a failed or stale source is a normal day, the last price kept and marked. Split from MA-42 and MA-151: the fuller chains come in phase 10 (MA-42) and phase 19 (MA-151).
  • Investment kinds (MA-206): shares, ETFs, OEICs, mutual funds, bonds and gold in the UK and India, inside their accounts (ISA, GIA, SIPP, demat, a mutual fund folio) as child records; the ISIN, ticker or AMFI scheme code found from a factsheet, a screenshot or a name; units and cost recorded; joint shares where there are two owners.
  • Plain-English transactions (MA-38): "I put £200 into Balanced" via a strict propose_change tool and Confirm.
  • XIRR to the penny (MA-212, ARCHITECTURE.md §6.6): for a holding, an account, a member and the household, from the dated ledger, against the old tracker's cases (MA-105); nothing returned, with the reason, when it cannot be computed. Split from MA-94.
  • No history, no invented return (MA-215): a holding with no ledger is flagged, and the agent asks for past transactions (typed, a statement or a CAS) or an estimate; an estimate is labelled wherever the return shows and can be corrected in place.
  • SIPs and regular contributions (MA-216): commitments that write a buy, not spending, for each instalment, with an annual step-up; the check-in asks whether it went through, was skipped or was stopped.
  • The Portfolio card (MA-36): the household's and each member's holdings as a card at both doors, valued from the day's prices, with the Household / Me switch and, for the owner, a tab per member.
  • The Assets and one-asset screens (MA-217, ARCHITECTURE.md §9.3, §12): Assets filtered by kind, member, country and maturity window; one asset with its value series, XIRR with its working, its events and files, and in-place correction of its editable figures, at both doors.

Tests: the plugin contract suite on recorded responses for yfinance and AMFI (pounds, not pence; the stale rule; idempotence per date; a clean failure when the source is down); XIRR against spreadsheet fixtures to the penny, the old tracker's cases included, and the reason given when it cannot be computed, with hypothesis; a typed transaction through Confirm; a fixture per investment kind, each holding inside its account; an estimate labelled wherever the return shows; a SIP instalment written as a buy, with its step-up; the Assets and one-asset screens at both doors.

Exit: an ISA fund, an Indian mutual fund and a share are registered through the chat and priced every day; each shows its XIRR with its working, or, with no history, the agent asks for past transactions or an estimate.

Cost: £0 for prices: yfinance and AMFI's NAV file are free; nothing more in AWS.
