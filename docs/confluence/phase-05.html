Shares, funds, bonds and gold in the UK and India, each inside its account, priced every day from free sources, with its real return: XIRR and CAGR from the dated ledger. Prices exist only to value what the household holds.

  • The PriceSource contract (MA-41) of ARCHITECTURE.md §5.1: one plugin per feed (yfinance, twelvedata, eodhd, issuer_page, ft, screenshot, stale, and amfi for India), a registry that imports only the plugins in use, per-plugin identifiers on the instrument, and chains per kind set in infra/<env>/terraform.tfvars (and a .env for local). yfinance is the default chain head in every environment; the EODHD plugin ships with the others, switched on by putting it first in the chain and adding its key.
  • Daily prices, free (MA-207): each holding priced every day from yfinance for listed lines and AMFI's NAV file for Indian mutual funds; London lines quoted in pence normalised to pounds; a failed or stale source is a normal day, the last price kept and marked. Split from MA-42 and MA-151: the fuller chain follows below (MA-42) and India's in phase 14 (MA-151).
  • The price chain (MA-42): yfinance (0P…L ids for the funds, tickers for ETFs), Twelve Data for the US lines, the screenshot plugin and the stale rule; ECB FX and calendars from phase 3 (MA-44). yfinance is unofficial and can break without notice, so the ingest job treats a failed or stale source as a normal day: it falls through the chain, marks the valuation provisional, and the 07:45 alarm fires only when every source has failed for two days.
  • Backups for the free feeds (MA-43): the BlackRock product page, the FT tearsheet and the EODHD plugin. EODHD is not in the chain unless the free sources are shown missing or misreporting London closes.
  • Input hygiene (MA-45): interpolated bars, provisional closes and stale prices dropped or marked before any holding is valued.
  • The source-comparison report (MA-46): when two plugins are configured for an instrument, both are recorded and the Activity page lists disagreements; yfinance is run against EODHD's free trial for the first month and the choice made from the numbers. Which source actually serves the Monzo funds' NAV is verified and recorded in docs/RESEARCH.md.
  • Investment kinds (MA-206): shares, ETFs, OEICs, mutual funds, bonds and gold in the UK and India, inside their accounts (ISA, GIA, SIPP, demat, a mutual fund folio) as child records; the ISIN, ticker or AMFI scheme code found from a factsheet, a screenshot or a name; units and cost recorded; joint shares where there are two owners.
  • Plain-English transactions (MA-38): "I put £200 into Balanced" via a strict propose_change tool and Confirm.
  • XIRR to the penny (MA-212, ARCHITECTURE.md §6): for a holding, an account, a member and the household, from the dated ledger, against the old tracker's cases (MA-105); nothing returned, with the reason, when it cannot be computed. Split from MA-94.
  • No history, no invented return (MA-215): a holding with no ledger is flagged, and the agent asks for past transactions (typed, a statement or a CAS) or an estimate; an estimate is labelled wherever the return shows and can be corrected in place.
  • SIPs and regular contributions (MA-216): commitments that write a buy, not spending, for each instalment, with an annual step-up; the check-in asks whether it went through, was skipped or was stopped.
  • The Portfolio card (MA-36): the household's and each member's holdings as a card at both doors, valued from the day's prices, with the Household / Me switch and, for an admin, a tab per member.
  • The Assets and one-asset screens (MA-217, ARCHITECTURE.md §8.3, §11): Assets filtered by kind, member, country and maturity window; one asset with its value series, XIRR with its working, its events and files, and in-place correction of its editable figures, at both doors.
  • What changed and why (MA-315, ARCHITECTURE.md §6.2, §7.1, §21.2): the Attribution view splits any change in net worth, by member, record, class and country, into saved, markets, currency, accrual, revaluations, corrections and other, worked out day by day so the parts add up to the penny, with where the saving went beside it; explain_change returns them; the claim check holds every "because" in a reply to a part a tool returned. The Overview's "where growth comes from" reads it in phase 9 (MA-95); designed first.

Tests: the plugin contract suite, run against every plugin on recorded responses (same Bars shape, pounds not pence, the stale rule, idempotence per date, a clean failure when the source is down), with the recordings refreshed only by a deliberate command; the chain falling through to stale; input hygiene on recorded bad bars; the source-comparison report on two recorded feeds; XIRR against spreadsheet fixtures to the penny, the old tracker's cases included, and the reason given when it cannot be computed, with hypothesis; a typed transaction through Confirm; a fixture per investment kind, each holding inside its account; an estimate labelled wherever the return shows; a SIP instalment written as a buy, with its step-up; the Assets and one-asset screens at both doors, including local Maestro p05 Photos addMedia valuation screenshot extraction and XIRR Technical fold (MA-273).
