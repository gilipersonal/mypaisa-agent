ARCHITECTURE.md §20, epic MA-147. Indian mutual funds, NSE and BSE shares and ETFs valued beside the UK holdings, free sources first, and the household's existing Indian portfolio loaded once with its whole history. The minimal AMFI NAV pricing landed in phase 5 (MA-207), so Indian mutual funds are valued every day from then; IN1 adds the history and the other sources.

  • IN1, Indian prices (MA-151). The amfi, mfapi and bhavcopy plugins; yfinance with .NS and .BO; the kinds in_mutual_fund and in_listed; INR from the ECB with the RBI/FBIL rate behind it; NSE's holiday calendar; jobs after the Indian close.
  • IN2, the import (MA-152). The detailed CAS from CAMS, KFintech or MF Central, the NSDL or CDSL CAS, and a broker's holdings and tradebook export; parsed in code, matched by ISIN, a dry run, one summary card, Confirm with the second check, rollback by import id; every file kept (MA-148); no PAN, address or account number in any record.
  • IN3, accounts and views (MA-153). demat, mf_folio, nre, nro; INR with GBP beside it; a tax flag where UK rules differ (the reporting-fund flag); rupees and pounds checked against each holding's currency both ways (MA-156); no suggestions; designed first.

Tests: synthetic CAS statements and broker exports import exactly once; an unmatched ISIN is listed, not guessed; each Indian chain falls through to stale; INR and GBP views agree with the FX of the day; local Maestro p14 Local File Provider CAMS/KFintech CAS PDF import behind Face ID and INR/GBP demat/folio views (MA-273).

Exit: an admin sends a member's CAS and broker export, taps Confirm once, and the Indian holdings with their history appear on the dashboard in INR and GBP, with the files they came from open from the Audit page.

Cost: £0 on the free path; a paid feed only on the admin's approval.
