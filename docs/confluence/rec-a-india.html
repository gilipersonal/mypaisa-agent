22. Indian markets: shares, ETFs and mutual funds (phase 19, later)

Arun, 24 Oct 2026 (MA-147): "If later I want to add Indian market in the mix will it work? ... I also have some existing portfolio of MF and stock in India market which I will feed later." It works without changing the core: currency, country and the kind of holding are data (§4), every feed is a plugin in a chain (§5.1), and the INR view comes in phase 3. The minimal pricing comes earlier, in phase 5 (MA-207): AMFI's daily NAV file for each Indian mutual fund the household registers, so the whole-wealth dashboard values them. This phase adds the rest: mfapi.in's history, NSE and BSE shares and ETFs, the other sources, two kinds, account types, a macro basket and an importer.

Two new kinds: in_mutual_fund, one NAV a day and no candles, like a UK OEIC; and in_listed, an NSE or BSE share or ETF with daily candles. NSE and BSE close at 15:30 IST (10:00 or 11:00 UK time) and AMFI's NAVs arrive by the UK evening, so the jobs run on the work queue after those times and the 07:15 brief carries India's previous day. NSE's trading holidays come from the exchange's calendar.

22.2 The existing Indian portfolio, imported once (IN2, MA-152)

What the member holdsThe file to sendWhat it gives
Mutual funds, every fund houseThe detailed Consolidated Account Statement (CAS) PDF from CAMS, KFintech or MF CentralEvery folio and scheme, units, and every purchase, SIP, switch and redemption since inception, so cost and XIRR are right from day one
Shares, ETFs and funds in dematThe NSDL or CDSL CAS, or the broker's holdings and tradebook export (for example Zerodha Console's CSV)Holdings and quantities by ISIN, and the trades from the tradebook
Anything elseA screenshot or a typed lineOne holding at a time, as for the UK funds

The files are parsed by code (an open-source CAS parser, pinned and tested on synthetic statements); Claude reads only a file the parsers cannot. Each holding is matched by ISIN: a fund to its AMFI scheme code, a share or ETF to its .NS or .BO listing; anything unmatched is listed, never guessed. The import is made safe on its own: a dry run, one summary card, fingerprints so the same statement never adds twice, Confirm with the second check, batch writes through the write service under one import id, rollback by that id, and an audit row per change that names the file it came from. The file itself is kept in S3 like every other (§4.6, MA-148). A protected PDF's password is typed once into the app, which opens the file on the device and sends the statement's lines with the original; the password is never stored, logged or sent; or the member sends an unlocked copy. The records never keep the PAN, the address or an account number, though the file is kept as sent.

22.3 Accounts, currency and suggestions (IN3, MA-153)

New account types demat, mf_folio, nre and nro (and ppf, nps, elss if one is held), each with country IN and currency INR. Every Indian figure shows in INR with its GBP value beside it; the household total is in the base currency with the INR view one tap away (phase 3). Rupees typed against a pound holding, or pounds against an Indian one, get the correction of §4.7 (MA-156). Privacy mode hides rupees as it hides pounds. "ISA-safe" becomes "wrapper-aware": a suggestion names only actions that suit the account it is about, and MyPaisa still never trades. For a UK resident many Indian funds are not UK "reporting funds", so gains may be taxed differently; MyPaisa flags those holdings in plain words and says to check with a tax adviser. It never gives tax advice. The cards and pages are designed first.

22.4 The engine and the Forecaster (IN4, MA-154)

An India basket joins the UK, EU and US ones in the macro pillar (§6.2): the Nifty 50 against Indian government bonds, India VIX, the rupee against the dollar, foreign portfolio investor flows and the RBI's policy rate. The news scout reads the Indian feeds, and the exposure maps come from the fund houses' monthly disclosures. The calendars add the RBI's policy dates, India's inflation releases, NSE holidays and results season. The Forecaster (§19) and its lab take the Indian holdings on the same terms: nothing is shown until its gates are passed.

22.5 Cost and order

On the free path India costs nothing beyond a few more jobs a day inside the free tiers. A paid feed is a request to the admin with its price, counted inside the £50 cap (§15.2). IN1 comes first; IN2 needs IN1 and file keeping (MA-148), and brings its own import safety (§22.2); IN3 needs both; IN4 needs IN1 and, for the Forecaster, phase 17.
