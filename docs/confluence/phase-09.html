The household's whole wealth on prod, complete at every door, and the old tracker at mypaisapp.com switched off.

  • The Overview, complete (MA-95, ARCHITECTURE.md §12): the hero (net worth in GBP or INR, switchable per member, with the ECB rate and its date, and the change since last month); liquid now, at maturity, and locked-in upside; where it sits; what is owed, and how it runs off; this month's cash flow; invested against owed, and where they cross; by member, and by country; maturing next; where growth comes from; the largest assets; and what is due (EMIs, maturities, open check-ins); from the old tracker's screen list (MA-105), at every door.
  • Reports, exports and the cutover (MA-97): composition by type and institution, exports as GET routes; at cutover the apex mypaisapp.com redirects to dashboard.mypaisapp.com, the old tracker's dev. and prod distributions are retired, and the zone moves from the old tracker's Terraform to this repository's.
  • History import (MA-111, ARCHITECTURE.md §18.5): one versioned Excel template sent in the chat, a dry run, Confirm with the second check, batch writes through the write service with audit rows, a fingerprint so a file sent twice adds nothing, and rollback by import id. Tested on dev's synthetic data first, then used to hydrate prod with the past investments XIRR needs. Prod is filled only through the app: the chat, statements and this importer.
  • Any question about the money (MA-104, ARCHITECTURE.md §12.1): a nightly export of the household's events and views to Parquet in S3, and a query_ledger_sql tool that runs read-only DuckDB over that household's files only, for questions the structured query_ledger tool (MA-62) cannot express; the figures come from the result, and the SQL is shown under Technical; designed first.

Tests: the importer (a wrong template version, a bad row, the same file twice, a rollback); every Overview figure matching the screen behind it; the viewer's masked figures; the INR and GBP views of the same household agreeing to the penny at the day's rate; query_ledger_sql reading only that household's files; the apex redirect and the old distributions' retirement in the Terraform plan.

Exit: the household's whole wealth is on prod, every figure entered through the chat, statements or the Excel importer, and corrected in place where needed; mypaisapp.com opens the dashboard, and the old tracker is switched off.
