The household's whole wealth on prod, complete at every door, and the old tracker at mypaisapp.com switched off.

  • The Overview, complete (MA-95, ARCHITECTURE.md §12): the hero (net worth in GBP, INR, USD or EUR, switchable per member, with the ECB rate and its date, and the change since last month); liquid now, at maturity, and locked-in upside; where it sits; what is owed, and how it runs off; this month's cash flow; invested against owed, and where they cross; by member, and by country; maturing next; where growth comes from; the largest assets; and what is due (EMIs, maturities, open check-ins); from the old tracker's screen list (MA-105), at every door.
  • Reports, exports and the cutover (MA-97): composition by type and institution, exports as GET routes; at cutover the apex mypaisapp.com redirects to dashboard.mypaisapp.com, the old tracker's dev. and prod distributions are retired, and the zone moves from the old tracker's Terraform to this repository's.
  • History import (MA-111, ARCHITECTURE.md §18.5): one versioned Excel template sent in the chat, from the app, the web app, or Claude Code on your laptop through a one-time upload link you open in the app (your pick, 29 Oct 2026), a dry run, Confirm with the second check, batch writes through the write service with audit rows, a fingerprint so a file sent twice adds nothing, and rollback by import id. Tested on dev's synthetic data first, then used to hydrate prod with the past investments XIRR needs. Prod is filled only through the app: the chat, statements and this importer.
  • Any question about the money (MA-104, ARCHITECTURE.md §12.1): a nightly export of the household's events and views to Parquet in S3, and a query_ledger_sql tool that runs read-only DuckDB over that household's files only, for questions the structured query_ledger tool (MA-62) cannot express; the figures come from the result, and the SQL is shown under Technical; designed first.
  • The household starts to learn, straight after the whole wealth (your pick, 29 Oct 2026; ARCHITECTURE.md §20.2, §20.9). Ahead of the market side, so the Budget forecast and the Projection learn from your own months as early as they can:
    • E1, settings (MA-129). Every behaviour a versioned Setting with a schema, a range, an automatic band and an approval rule; defaults in config/defaults.yaml; one settings service; a minute's cache; a daily snapshot to S3.
    • E9, the household's forecasts graded and tuned (MA-276, ARCHITECTURE.md §20.9). At each month close, that month's line of the Budget forecast is graded against what happened, each gap traced to its input. The tuner proposes a category's run rate, a category's own inflation or a member's salary growth from at least 6 closed months, a step at a time, and the admin's Confirm applies it as a new version of the assumptions; the records, the plan and the arithmetic never move. The Budget forecast tab shows each month's forecast beside its actual; designed first.
    • E10, each member's needs learned from use (MA-277, ARCHITECTURE.md §20.9). From each member's own taps, answers, corrections and mutes, never from text a file or a page brought in: the composer's chips and the brief's sections ordered on their own inside a band; a merchant's category, an alert threshold, a quiet hour or a usual answer proposed as a card for that member's Confirm; a field corrected after a file kept as a case; You lists what was learned, with a reset; designed first. E10 starts with what exists by then (the chat's chips, the merchant memory, check-in answers, corrections after a file) and learns the brief and the alerts once phases 11 and 12 bring them.

Tests: the importer (a wrong template version, a bad row, the same file twice, a rollback); every Overview figure matching the screen behind it; the viewer's masked figures; the GBP, INR, USD and EUR views of the same household agreeing to the penny at the day's rate; an agent unable to move a setting outside its band (MA-129); a month's grade read back the same from its stored run, no assumption proposed before 6 closed months or applied without the admin's Confirm, text a file or a page brought in teaching E10 nothing, and one member's learning never reaching another (MA-276, MA-277); query_ledger_sql reading only that household's files; the apex redirect and the old distributions' retirement in the Terraform plan; local Maestro p09 whole-wealth cross-band sweep (320 to 1376 pt), Dynamic Type AX5, Dark 25%-brightness assertScreenshot and Local File Provider Excel workbook upload (MA-273).
