05 · The 07:15 morning brief, end to end

At 07:00 the prices come in from free sources; by 07:10 the engine has scored every holding; at 07:15 each member's brief arrives: in the chat if the app is open, and as a notification if it is not. If a price is late, the brief still goes, and says so.

Trigger EventBridge Scheduler, Europe/London, each weekday Phase 10 (prices, engine) Phase 11 (the brief)

📌 The morning, by the clock

07:00Ingest. The Scheduler queues one job per holding; free sources first.

07:10Signals. The engine has scored every holding; stances stay in shadow until phase 15.

07:15Brief. Once for the household and once for each member who wants one, in their own preferences: in the chat, and pushed to a closed app.

07:45Dead man's switch. If the brief has not gone out, an alarm tells the owner, in the app and by push.

  • The lock screen. The brief as an ordinary notification: the moves, never an amount, unless you turn amounts on.
  • The owner's brief. The household, a line for Asha, the chart drawn in the card from the engine's series.
  • Labelled, not skipped. A late price goes out marked stale; privacy mode hides pounds, in the strip too, but keeps the moves.

🛡️ Safeguards on this path

Claude never produces a number

The validator catches an invented figure; a refusal or error falls back to the plain template. The chart is drawn from the engine's series, not from words.

Stale is labelled, not skipped

The previous close, marked stale and kept out of scoring. The brief, the wrap and alerts are always attempted.

Retries and dead-letter queues

Each job: five tries, backoff from 30 seconds to 30 minutes, then its dead-letter queue. Idempotent per date.

Free sources kept inside their limits

The work queue's concurrency keeps Twelve Data's free tier at 8 calls a minute; ingest spends at most 200 of its 800 daily credits.

The dead man's switch

07:45: no brief, an alarm. Any holding's price older than two days also raises one.

No amounts on the lock screen

A notification shows moves, not money, unless the member turns on "Show amounts in notifications"; with privacy mode on, never. In the app, privacy mode hides pounds, and rupees once Indian holdings arrive.

The kill switch

/pause sets a flag every scheduled run checks first.

🛡️ Safeguards on this path

Tiers, as interruption levels

Red is time-sensitive with sound, amber is passive, the rest goes in a digest. About ten messages a week, two with sound at most.

Cooldowns and quiet hours

Per member, enforced by the agent Lambda before anything is pushed, never by the prompt.

Two reds a week

The weekly red budget is per member; an alert never fires twice in its cooldown.

No repeats

Haiku's repeat filter, and a self-audit of what MyPaisa said this week.

Never dropped

At any step of the spend ladder an alert goes out; a template needs no model.

The app cannot trade

The card suggests only ISA-safe actions and never says sell everything.
