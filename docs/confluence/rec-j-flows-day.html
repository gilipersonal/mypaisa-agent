05 · The daily prices, end to end

At 07:00 each holding's price comes in from free sources; by 07:10 every holding is valued again, and Home, each holding's screen, its XIRR and the household's net worth read the new figures. Prices are kept only to value what the household holds: nothing here scores, ranks or predicts a holding. If a price is late, the last one stays, marked stale.

Trigger EventBridge Scheduler, Europe/London, each weekday Phase 5 (prices, free) Phase 13 (the data steward)

📌 The morning, by the clock

07:00Ingest. The Scheduler queues one job per holding; free sources first.

07:10Valued. Every holding has the day's price, or its last one marked stale, and its value in its own currency and in pounds.

07:45Dead man's switch. If the price jobs have not finished, an alarm tells the owner, in the app and by push.

🛡️ Safeguards on this path

Prices value holdings, nothing more

A price sets a holding's value and, through it, its XIRR and the net worth. Nothing scores, ranks or predicts a holding, and nothing says buy or sell.

Stale is labelled, not skipped

The previous close, marked stale with its date, until a source answers. A figure is never shown as newer than it is.

Retries and dead-letter queues

Each job: five tries, backoff from 30 seconds to 30 minutes, then its dead-letter queue. Idempotent per date.

Free sources kept inside their limits

The work queue's concurrency keeps Twelve Data's free tier at 8 calls a minute; ingest spends at most 200 of its 800 daily credits.

The dead man's switch

07:45: the price jobs not finished, an alarm. Any holding's price older than two days also raises one.

The kill switch

/pause sets a flag every scheduled run checks first.
