What the household owes, and the agent asking when something is due, so the records stay current with a tap, not a form.

  • Loan maths (MA-204, ARCHITECTURE.md §6): the EMI, the schedule, the interest and principal split, prepayment by term or by EMI, and preclosure, to the penny; events replayed in order (a prepayment, a rate change, a reset, a drawdown, a charge); the old tracker's amortisation cases as golden fixtures (MA-105). Split from MA-94.
  • Loans, mortgages and EMIs (MA-209): a home loan, a mortgage, a car or a personal loan, an EMI and buy-now-pay-later, in the UK and India, registered from a sentence; dated terms (a floating-rate reset, a fixed rate that ends); a prepayment shortens the term or lowers the EMI, by country default; a balance checkpoint starts a loan part-way through without its history; closure marks the record closed.
  • Credit cards (MA-210): each card kept as revolving debt from its statements (balance, due date, minimum); a card payment is a transfer, and only interest and fees count as spending; the statement's check-in asks paid in full, the minimum, or part.
  • Commitments and check-ins (MA-205, ARCHITECTURE.md §7): a Commitment is any expected, dated money tied to a record or a member (an EMI, a card bill, a SIP, rent, a premium, a salary, a maturity, a valuation due); a CheckIn is one occurrence, keyed by the commitment and its due date and created once. A daily worker job decides in code, with no LLM, and puts each check-in card on the outbox, with push (no amounts on the lock screen, MA-170): "HDFC home loan: was November's EMI paid?" with Paid, Not yet, Different amount and Paid off in full. The card shows exactly what a tap writes, so Paid is the Confirm, with Undo; Not yet asks again in a few days, then shows as overdue; Different amount and Paid off in full open a short flow, and Paid off in full closes the record and ends the commitment. Several due at once share one card with All paid. A tap writes a record, so check-ins go to the admins and only an admin answers one (MA-307); a member who is not an admin sees that one is pending, with no buttons. This replaces the one-off reminders.
  • Autopay and the weekly digest (MA-211): an autopay commitment is assumed paid after its grace period unless a statement or an admin says otherwise, and a weekly digest lists what was assumed, which keeps the agent to about ten notifications a week.
  • The Debts and one-loan screens (MA-214, ARCHITECTURE.md §8.3, §11): Debts shows the outstanding total, the EMI total and the interest left, by member; one loan shows its balance against its plan, the yearly split, the end date, prepayment what-ifs from the engine, its events and its files, at both doors.
