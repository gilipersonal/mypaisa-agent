MyPaisa reaches the household outside the app where iOS allows it (MA-175). Each story is designed first; privacy mode holds everywhere.

  • A widget (MA-176): the household's total and the day's move on the Home Screen and the Lock Screen, from the last fetch with its time; privacy mode shows the mark and "Hidden".
  • The share sheet (MA-177): a factsheet or a screenshot shared from Mail, Files or Photos goes to MyPaisa and opens the chat on the reading card; nothing is saved before Confirm.
  • Siri and Shortcuts (MA-178): "How is the portfolio today?" answered with the total and the day's move; "Log money into MyPaisa" opens the guided flow; never a write without Confirm in the app.
  • A Live Activity (MA-179): an import (MA-111, MA-152) followed on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, with no amounts.
  • Optional AWS VPC enterprise lockdown and data perimeter (MA-283, decided last): once Phase 20 is done, Arun decides whether to keep the £3.50/month serverless IAM/tag baseline or turn on enable_vpc_lockdown across dev and prod in us-east-1 (MA-284, docs/RESEARCH.md §9): preferred Option 3 (+£13.32/month in leanest 3A, or +£24.86/month in 3B with CloudFront Pro bundled AWS WAF on prod, keeping total AWS spend ~£16.82–£28.36/month inside the £50/month AWS cap, versus Option 2B £70.39/month, Option 2A £149.01/month and Option 1 £729.91/month) adds dual-stack VPCs with VPC Block Public Access, zero-egress Isolated subnets for deterministic and quarantined functions, free S3 and DynamoDB Gateway Endpoints with three-way aws:SourceVpce Data Perimeter Deny policies, IPv6 EIGW, auto-healing Graviton t4g.nano fck-nat / Envoy TLS-SNI egress proxies, Route 53 Resolver DNS Firewall default-deny allowlists, private arm64 CodeBuild runners, S3 v5 VPC Flow Logs and GuardDuty in a five-step zero-lockout rollout.

Tests: each widget, intent and the share extension from fixtures, in light, dark, tinted and clear, with privacy on; the extension signs in through the shared Keychain group and refuses when signed out; local Maestro p20 Safari/Files Share Sheet to MyPaisa Dev, App Intents deep links and WidgetKit snapshot checks (MA-273).

Cost: £0 (or +£13.32/month for Option 3A / +£24.86/month for preferred Option 3B if the optional MA-283 VPC lockdown is switched on last, staying inside the £50/month AWS cap).
