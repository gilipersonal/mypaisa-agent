MyPaisa reaches the household outside the app where iOS allows it (MA-175). Each story is designed first; privacy mode holds everywhere.

  • A widget (MA-176): the household's total and the day's move on the Home Screen and the Lock Screen, from the last fetch with its time; privacy mode shows the mark and "Hidden".
  • The share sheet (MA-177): a factsheet or a screenshot shared from Mail, Files or Photos goes to MyPaisa and opens the chat on the reading card; nothing is saved before Confirm.
  • Siri and Shortcuts (MA-178): "How is the portfolio today?" answered with the total and the day's move; "Log money into MyPaisa" opens the guided flow; never a write without Confirm in the app.
  • A Live Activity (MA-179): an import (MA-111, MA-152) followed on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, with no amounts.

Tests: each widget, intent and the share extension from fixtures, in light, dark, tinted and clear, with privacy on; the extension signs in through the shared Keychain group and refuses when signed out.

Exit: the widget on your Lock Screen shows the household's day without opening the app, and hides it when privacy mode is on.
