Claude writes on top of numbers that already work, and the household hears when something needs it: a budget running over, a check-in left open, a job that did not run.

  • Anthropic client with the key from SSM, model routing, caching, refusal fallback; alerts and cards through the figure check the chat has had since phase 1 (MA-54, MA-248).
  • The alert cards, designed first (MA-55): a budget category over its plan, a check-in overdue, a maturity or a valuation due, a failed job; the Technical section on every card; /technical mode; the glossary on a tap.
  • Charts: one house style, 1a Monzo Pulse (MA-295, MA-56), in Swift Charts and LayerChart; a holding's value series, the net worth curve, the budget against its plan and the Projection's fan, drawn in the card from the views' series (MA-58).
  • Alert tiers, cooldowns, weekly red budget, quiet hours, Haiku repeat filter; alert tiers as push interruption levels (red time-sensitive with sound, amber passive) (MA-59); dead man's switch and error alarms to the owner, in the app and by push (MA-61).
  • The ladder's lower steps (MA-114): at 85% of the ceiling web searches are limited to three a day; at 95% chat runs on Haiku and alerts go out as templates; alerts are never dropped.
  • Quiet nudges and a Monday digest (MA-316, ARCHITECTURE.md §10.1): a nightly insight job with detectors in code (budget drift, idle cash, a goal off its glide path, a maturity or a rate change coming, a prepayment's impact, an unusual spend, a salary not credited, a rupee move, an allowance's year end), each finding scored and fingerprinted; one silent digest card on Monday morning per admin and at most one timely nudge a week; muted kinds learned by E10; facts only, never "should"; designed first.

Tests: the figure check catching an invented number in an alert and a card; alert state machine with frozen time; spend driven through each step of the ladder with frozen time, checking which calls are made and on which model; card rendering from recorded Claude responses; chart snapshot tests and local Maestro p10 alert cards, time-sensitive red alert push, Swift Charts AXChartDescriptor summary and Technical glossary tap (MA-273).

Exit: two weeks of alerts in which none fires twice in its cooldown; a layman reads the top of a card and understands it without the bottom.

Cost: about £3.60 a month, the insight job's lines and its Monday digest about £0.60 of it (ARCHITECTURE.md §14).
