MyPaisa keeps one household's whole wealth across the UK and India, registered and kept through a chat, with no forms: what the family owns and owes, each member's income and spending, a Projection of where it is heading from the household's actual numbers (XIRR and CAGR), and a team of agents that keeps it all current. Only admins write, through the chat; every other member views. MyPaisa gives no buy, sell or hold calls and never predicts a market (MA-307). Every phase ships at both doors at once: the web app (a SvelteKit 3 PWA at dashboard.mypaisapp.com, desktop and phone) and the iOS app (a React Native shell with a native SwiftUI core, iPhone and iPad).
