Where the household's net worth is heading over the years, computed by code and steered by the agent. Three words, three engines (ARCHITECTURE.md §6.6, MA-275): the stock investment forecast is the market's, real prediction graded against prices (MA-52, the Forecaster); the Budget forecast is deterministic, worked out from the household's inputs to the penny (MA-262); the Projection is the household's net worth over time, the same path and then a fan; and ForecastAssumptions becomes ProjectionAssumptions.

  • The engine (MA-219, ARCHITECTURE.md §6.6): wealth/projection.py runs month by month to a horizon (twenty years by default; 5, 10, 20 or 30), with a today's-money toggle, money in Decimal. Its inputs: each record's country, which sets its inflation and wage-growth prior, each currency drifting against GBP with its inflation gap; each member's current net salary, its growth blending the member's own measured history with the country's prior, weighted by the years of history; returns (contractual rates as given; a market asset's observed XIRR shrunk toward a long-run prior per asset class and country, by how much history it has; estimates as marked); debts amortising to their real end dates and freeing their EMIs; maturities, commitments, the budget's spend inflating by country, and the admin's plan items; the surplus allocated by a rule; and no locked pension covering a shortfall before it unlocks. The deterministic path first, then a Monte Carlo fan (p10, p50, p90); each run stored as a ProjectionRun with its inputs hash, seed, engine version, percentiles and milestones. It ports the old tracker's forecast.ts, budget.ts and amortisation.ts with their cases as golden fixtures, then extends them. Since MA-261 it also applies a plan item's three effects (a new flow; a change to an existing flow, such as a salary, a SIP or a budget category, from a date; an event on a record, such as a loan prepaid or closed, freeing its EMI), steps growth up on each anniversary of a plan's start, pays a one-off from where it names (the surplus and cash first when it names nothing, a flag if they run short), and puts out a budget forecast ledger and a trace of the flows behind every figure.
  • Priors by country (MA-221): long-run returns per asset class and country, inflation per country with category overrides such as education and health, and wage growth per country, each versioned with its source; set by the admin, every change audited.
  • Plan items: future plans by talking (MA-222, MA-261, ARCHITECTURE.md §4.1, §4.5, §4.10, §6.6, §8): the admin says what is coming ("£4,000 a month from Jan 2029 for my kid's schooling", "my salary goes to £8,000 a month from Sep 2028", "₹2 cr to close my SBI home loan"); the agent asks only for what is missing, at most three questions with chips (whose, until when, how it grows, paid from where), and shows an impact card from the engine (net worth at 5, 10 and 20 years, the surplus each year, the first month cash would go negative, new milestones) for one Confirm. One-off or recurring, with a start, an end, a currency and a growth rate; every amount take-home. Created only in the admin's chat and corrected, paused or ended in place only by the admin, as are the saved assumptions; members see them; every change audited with the admin's own words and the Projection run before and after; no second check, since they change a plan, not the records.
  • The Budget forecast (MA-262, ARCHITECTURE.md §6.6, §9.3, §12): each year to the horizon, expandable to its months: income by member, spend by category, EMIs, each plan item, the surplus, the savings rate and closing cash, in GBP, INR, USD or EUR; any figure opens the flows that make it; the months below the admin's floor flagged by code; at both doors.
  • Rearranging the budget (MA-263, ARCHITECTURE.md §4.5, §4.10, §6.6, §8, §12): when a plan leaves cash below zero or the surplus below the admin's floor (a monthly surplus kept among the Projection's assumptions, £500 to start, the admin's alone, holding while anyone in the household earns a salary; in retirement only cash running out is flagged; Arun, 28 Oct 2026, MA-264), code works out the options (trim a category from a date, pause a SIP, move a plan's start, draw from a named pool, change the surplus rule) and the agent ranks and explains them, each as an unsaved scenario card; the admin's Confirm applies one, as Claude proposes a change and you accept it: plan items from their dates and, where it touches this month, the current month's budget plan, each with its audit row and Undo.
  • What-ifs and the story (MA-223, ARCHITECTURE.md §6.6, §8): code produces every number; the model never does. The agent proposes the assumption set with a reason for each figure; answers a what-if ("what if we buy a house in 2028?") by filling a typed override whose numbers trace to the member's words or a named preset, shown as an unsaved scenario card; explains what drives the curve, from the engine's trace; and raises the trouble flags and milestones code computes. Members ask what-ifs but save nothing.
  • The Projection screen (MA-224, ARCHITECTURE.md §6.6, §9.3, §12): the fan in GBP, INR, USD or EUR, in today's money or not, opening at 20 years with 5, 10, 20 and 30 to choose; milestones on the line (loans ending, maturities, plan items); the assumptions panel with each source, editable in place by the admin; a Plans tab (each plan on a timeline, corrected in place by the admin, with a pause toggle and Add in chat) and a Budget forecast tab; scenario chips; Ask; at both doors.

Tests: the engine against the old tracker's forecast and budget cases as golden fixtures; debts amortising to their real end dates; a locked pension never covering a shortfall before it unlocks; the shrinkage weight on hand-computed cases; a Monte Carlo run reproduced from its stored seed; the override validator refusing a number traced to neither the member's words nor a named preset; plan items and saved assumptions refused to a member, in the chat and in place; golden fixtures for the three sentences above (the schooling, the salary step, the loan closed and its EMI freed); every month, opening cash plus its flows equals closing cash; a paused plan changes nothing; the goal-seek's options on hand-computed cases; eval cases for the three sentences and ambiguous ones (no end date, "about 2 cr", a loan that does not exist); the Projection screen at both doors, including local Maestro p07 fan scrub, 1a Budget forecast, 2a chat plan rearrangement and 3a Plans tab on iPhone 402 and iPad 1376 (MA-273).
