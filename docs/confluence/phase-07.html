Where the household's net worth is heading over the years, computed by code and steered by the agent. Forecast stays the market's word (MA-52, the Forecaster); Projection is the household's net worth over time, and ForecastAssumptions becomes ProjectionAssumptions.

  • The engine (MA-219, ARCHITECTURE.md §6.6): wealth/projection.py runs month by month to a horizon (thirty years by default), with a today's-money toggle. Its inputs: each record's country, which sets its inflation, wage-growth prior and tax drag, with GBP/INR drifting with the inflation gap; each member's current net salary, its growth blending the member's own measured history with the country's prior, weighted by the years of history; returns (contractual rates as given; a market asset's observed XIRR shrunk toward a long-run prior per asset class and country, by how much history it has; estimates as marked); debts amortising to their real end dates and freeing their EMIs; maturities, commitments, the budget's spend inflating by country, and the admin's plan items; the surplus allocated by a rule; and no locked pension covering a shortfall before it unlocks. The deterministic path first, then a Monte Carlo fan (p10, p50, p90); each run stored as a ProjectionRun with its inputs hash, seed, engine version, percentiles and milestones. It ports the old tracker's forecast.ts, budget.ts and amortisation.ts with their cases as golden fixtures, then extends them.
  • Priors by country (MA-221): long-run returns per asset class and country, inflation per country with category overrides such as education and health, and wage growth per country, each versioned with its source; set by the admin, every change audited.
  • Plan items (MA-222): future one-off or recurring income and spends, with a start, an end, a currency and a growth rate, created only in the admin's chat and corrected in place only by the admin, as are the saved assumptions; members see them; every change audited; no second check, since they change a plan, not the records.
  • What-ifs and the story (MA-223): code produces every number; the model never does. The agent proposes the assumption set with a reason for each figure; answers a what-if ("what if we buy a house in 2028?") by filling a typed override whose numbers trace to the member's words or a named preset, shown as an unsaved scenario card; explains what drives the curve; and raises the trouble flags and milestones code computes. Members ask what-ifs but save nothing.
  • The Projection screen (MA-224, ARCHITECTURE.md §9.3, §12): the fan in GBP or INR, in today's money or not; milestones on the line (loans ending, maturities, plan items); the assumptions panel with each source, editable in place by the admin; scenario chips; Ask; at both doors.

Tests: the engine against the old tracker's forecast and budget cases as golden fixtures; debts amortising to their real end dates; a locked pension never covering a shortfall before it unlocks; the shrinkage weight on hand-computed cases; a Monte Carlo run reproduced from its stored seed; the override validator refusing a number traced to neither the member's words nor a named preset; plan items and saved assumptions refused to a member, in the chat and in place; the Projection screen at both doors.

Exit: the Projection screen shows the fan with its milestones; you ask a what-if and see an unsaved scenario; as the admin you add a plan item through the chat and correct an assumption in place, each with its audit row.

Cost: the engine is code; only the assumptions, the what-ifs and the narration call Claude, inside the ceiling; nothing more in AWS.
