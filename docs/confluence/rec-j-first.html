📟 How a layman uses the bot, scene by scene

Eight journeys through the Telegram bot, played on a phone. MyPaisa is a wealth tracker driven by the chat: it keeps the household's records, its budget and its wealth forecast from the household's own numbers. Each journey shows what you tap, what comes back, and what happens behind it. Nothing technical appears unless you ask; when you do, it is one tap away. These are mock-ups of the design; nothing is built yet.

Kept for reference. Eight user journeys on a phone frame, in the Nocturne look, from 23 Oct 2026 (journeys.html). Superseded by the Agent Journey (its twenty-four flows); the Telegram bot they show was superseded by the iOS app (MA-161), and the web app was kept beside it.

🗓️ Rules every journey keeps

Plain on top, detail on tap

Every card reads in two lines. "Technical ▾" opens the numbers; a glossary sits behind each term.

Only admins write

The household can have several admins. Admins add and delete only in the chat, with a card and Confirm; a delete asks twice, marks the record deleted (restorable through the chat) and writes an audit row. Admins correct a figure in the chat or in place on a screen. Every other member views only.

Numbers from code, words from Claude

Every figure in a message came out of a tool; Claude only chooses what to say.

Forecasts from your own numbers

The Projection and the Budget forecast come from the household's actual numbers: XIRR and CAGR from the dated ledger, salaries, debts and plans. Nothing predicts a market; nothing says buy or sell.

It cannot trade

No broker login exists. Daily prices only value what you hold.

Silence is a feature

Alerts only for a budget line, a check-in due or a failed job; a weekly budget of two loud ones, quiet hours, and a filter that drops repeats.
