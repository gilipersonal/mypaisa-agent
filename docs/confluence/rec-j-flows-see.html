06 · Asking a question in chat

Ask in plain words. The agent looks up what it needs with its tools, sends Claude only the current conversation, and answers with a card; its words stream into the chat as they are written. Any member may ask about the household's wealth. Why? explains a move in value from code's own figures: the FX change and the holding's currency split. Nothing searches the web to explain a move, nothing predicts a market, and nothing says buy or sell.

Trigger a typed question, the Ask chip, or a screen's Ask Phase 1 (memory, streaming) Phase 11 (tools, cards)

📌 Chat memory: keep it all, send little

Yesterday 19:02Asked what the household spent on eating out. Closed after 30 minutes of silence; Haiku wrote a one-line note.note

Today 08:13Logged £200 into Balanced. Closed when the guided flow finished.note

42 minutes of silence: a new conversation starts

Today 08:55"How has Balanced done since we started?" The only conversation sent to Claude, after the cached fixed part and the notes above.sent

Kept

Every message, one ChatTurn row each, stored exactly as returned, for ninety days. History is never edited; the app reads it from GET /v1/chat/turns.

Sent

The cached fixed part (system prompt, tools, card rubrics; one-hour cache), notes on recent conversations, and the current one.

Ends

After 30 minutes of silence or when a guided flow finishes; it stays open while a question waits. Past about 50,000 tokens it starts afresh from a summary.

Costs

About 1.5p to 3p a message on Sonnet, where re-sending whole histories would cost 7p to 85p after a month.

👀 What the member sees

  • Streaming. The words arrive as Claude writes them; the card follows whole, its figures from code.
  • The answer card. Its returns from the dated ledger; the working one tap away.
  • Why? and Technical. The move explained from code's figures; the return's working, from code.

🛡️ Safeguards on this path

Numbers from tools

Claude calls the tools and words the card; the value, XIRR and CAGR come from code, and a card's figures are typed numbers in its JSON, never prose. Nothing predicts a market or says buy or sell.

No web search to explain a move

Why? reads only code's figures. Web search only looks up a holding's public details when it is added, and its text is never obeyed. No tool can trade, erase data or message anyone else; a delete is an admin's draft, saved only on Confirm and the second check, and only marks the record deleted.

Short memory, full record

Only the current conversation is sent; the agent looks up the rest with get_audit and get_portfolio.

At most eight calls a run

A loop that reaches it stops and says so.

The spend ladder

At 85% web searches are limited to three a day; at 95% chat answers on Haiku; at 100% free text waits for the renewal.

Confirm before any write

A question can lead to a change, but only through a card and Confirm: a record by an admin, a member's own preference ("stop pinging me about check-ins") by that member.

07 · The dashboard in the app

Home, a holding with its value and XIRR, the audit log of every change, and what the agent did and spent: the same screens on iPhone and iPad, one tab away from the chat, and in the web app on a laptop (flow 9). Nothing new is created on them; an admin can correct an existing figure in place (rule 4), and every other member only reads. An admin's Update and every screen's Ask open the chat.

🛡️ Safeguards on this path

Admins write, through the chat

No screen, in the app or the web app, has a form that creates or deletes a record: adding and deleting go through the chat. An admin may correct an existing figure its kind marks editable in place, and the data routes accept nothing else; both go through one write service, with an audit row. Every other member views only. The one write path outside it is the admins' Admin center, for admin settings and operations only, each behind the second check; none changes a record.

Checked on every call

Token, status, epoch and role on every call; the token never leaves the Keychain except to the api.

Privacy mode

Pounds and rupees hidden on every screen and chart axis, with placeholders that stay visible in dark; the app switcher shows only the mark.

Roles

Only admins write. A member sees the household totals and their own detail and views only; a viewer reads Home and what members share.

The audit log, readable

Filtered by member, record and date; a member sees their own rows and the household's, an admin sees all.

Offline, labelled

The last Home, holdings and conversation stay on the device with complete file protection, shown "as of 08:42" when the network is gone; an old figure is never shown as new.

08 · On the iPad: reading Home while asking about it

On an iPad in landscape, or any window from about 1133 points, the chat sits beside every screen; from about 600 to 1133 points it is a sheet away, and in a compact window (every iPhone, a folded foldable, Slide Over) it is the Chat tab. You read Home and ask about it at once: the question carries the screen as its context, the answer streams in beside the figures, and Home stays where it was. The same conversation is on your iPhone.

09 · The web app on a laptop

The chat, the dashboard and the admins' Admin center also live in a browser: dashboard.mypaisapp.com, the web app, a SvelteKit 3 progressive web app that installs like an app on a laptop, an iPad or a phone. The same conversation, the same figures, the audit log of every change, and what the agent did and spent. An admin adds and deletes in its chat, as in the app, with a card and Confirm, and can correct an existing figure in place; every other member views.
