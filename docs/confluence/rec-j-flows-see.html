07 · Asking a question in chat

Ask in plain words. The agent looks up what it needs with its tools, sends Claude only the current conversation, and answers with a card; its words stream into the chat as they are written. Why? goes to the web for today's reasons, with the sources cited.

Trigger a typed question, the Ask chip, or a screen's Ask Phase 1 (memory, streaming) Phase 12 (tools, cards)

📌 Chat memory: keep it all, send little

Yesterday 19:02Asked about the NASDAQ 100 fall. Closed after 30 minutes of silence; Haiku wrote a one-line note.note

Today 08:13Logged £200 into Balanced. Closed when the guided flow finished.note

42 minutes of silence: a new conversation starts

Today 08:55"Should I add to Balanced this month?" The only conversation sent to Claude, after the cached fixed part and the notes above.sent

Kept

Every message, one ChatTurn row each, stored exactly as returned, for ninety days. History is never edited; the app reads it from GET /v1/chat/turns.

Sent

The cached fixed part (system prompt, tools, card rubrics; one-hour cache), notes on recent conversations, and the current one.

Ends

After 30 minutes of silence or when a guided flow finishes; it stays open while a question waits. Past about 50,000 tokens it starts afresh from a summary.

Costs

About 1.5p to 3p a message on Sonnet, where re-sending whole histories would cost 7p to 85p after a month.

🛤️ Safeguards on this path

Numbers from tools

Claude calls the tools and words the card; the stance, confidence and range come from the engine, and a card's figures are typed numbers in its JSON, never prose.

Untrusted web text

Search results that contain instructions are not acted on. No tool can trade, delete or message anyone else.

Short memory, full record

Only the current conversation is sent; the agent looks up the rest with get_audit, get_portfolio, get_stances.

At most eight calls a run

A loop that reaches it stops and says so.

The spend ladder

At 85% Why? gets three searches a day; at 95% chat answers on Haiku; at 100% free text waits for the renewal.

Confirm before any write

A question can lead to a change ("stop pinging me under 2%"), but only through a card and Confirm.

08 · The dashboard in the app

Home, a holding, Overlap and Scorecard, the audit log of every change, and what the agent did and spent: the same screens on iPhone and iPad, one tab away from the chat, and in the web app on a laptop (flow 10). Nothing new is created on them, and an existing figure can be corrected in place (rule 4); a screen's Update and Ask open the chat.

🛤️ Safeguards on this path

Create in the chat, correct in place

No screen, in the app or the web app, has a form that creates a record: anything new goes through the chat. An existing figure its kind marks editable can be corrected in place, and the data routes accept nothing else; both go through one write service, with an audit row. The one write path outside it is the owner's Admin center, for admin settings and operations only, each behind the second check; none changes a record.

Checked on every call

Token, status, epoch and role on every call; the token never leaves the Keychain except to the api.

Privacy mode

Pounds and rupees hidden on every screen and chart axis, with placeholders that stay visible in dark; the app switcher shows only the mark.

Roles

A member sees the household totals and their own detail; a viewer reads Home and what members share.

The audit log, readable

Filtered by member, record and date; a member sees their own rows and the household's, the owner sees all.

Offline, labelled

The last Home, holdings and conversation stay on the device with complete file protection, shown "as of 08:42" when the network is gone; an old figure is never shown as new.

09 · On the iPad: reading Home while asking about it

On an iPad in landscape, or any window from about 1133 points, the chat sits beside every screen; from about 600 to 1133 points it is a sheet away, and in a compact window (every iPhone, a folded foldable, Slide Over) it is the Chat tab. You read Home and ask about it at once: the question carries the screen as its context, the answer streams in beside the figures, and Home stays where it was. The same conversation is on your iPhone.

🛤️ Safeguards on this path

A pointer, never a figure

The screen's context tells the agent where you are; the answer's numbers come from the engine, as everywhere else.

One conversation, every device

Each finished message reaches every open stream of the member; a device with the app closed catches up on opening, so nothing depends on a stream staying up.

Created in the chat beside it

The screen creates nothing: anything new starts the guided flow in the chat column, a card, Confirm, an audit row. An existing figure can be corrected in place, through the same write service.

Nothing lost on a turn or a fold

Scene state restoration keeps the conversation, the scroll position and any guided flow through a rotation, a fold or a window resize.

Privacy in both columns

Privacy mode hides amounts in the screen and in the chat at once, and in the strip and the sidebar.

Readable at every size

Dynamic Type from xSmall to AX5 (figures stack under their labels from AX1), a spoken label on every figure, 44 pt targets, glass made opaque under Reduce Transparency.

10 · The web app on a laptop

The chat, the dashboard and the owner's Admin center also live in a browser: dashboard.mypaisapp.com, the web app, a SvelteKit 3 progressive web app that installs like an app on a laptop, an iPad or a phone. The same conversation, the same figures, the audit log of every change, and what the agent did and spent. Anything new is created in its chat, as in the app, with a card and Confirm; an existing figure can be corrected in place.

Trigger dashboard.mypaisapp.com in a browser, or the installed web app Phases 1 to 9 Phase 13 (Overlap, Scorecard)
