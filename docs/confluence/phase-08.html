Audit and Files, Search and the Admin center (members and devices, the second check, the ceiling, caps and alerts, replay), at both doors, at dashboard(-dev).mypaisapp.com and in the app. The dashboard's screens are built with their records in phases 2 to 9.

  • Built from the boards in MyPaisa iOS v1 and MyPaisa app v1 (docs/design/README.md), on iPhone, iPad, the foldable and in a browser; Warm ledger (MA-101) on Liquid Glass; Swift Charts in the app and shadcn-svelte's charts on LayerChart on the web.
  • Audit (MA-71, MA-79, MA-149): the §4.6 log, filtered by member, record and date, before and after, a link to the run, and the file behind a change opened through a five-minute link (Quick Look in the app), with a Files view, at every door. Activity, at every door from phase 1 (MA-196), gains the schedule board with last and next run and price freshness, this month's spend against the ceiling and the step it is on, and the last seven days of alarms (MA-71).
  • Search (MA-180): finds a record, a change, a file or a past answer from anywhere.
  • The Admin center's Spend section (MA-116), designed first like every screen: this cycle's spend by member, feature, agent and model against the ceiling, the step and the days left; an admin sets the ceiling (from $10 to the hard limit), the member share, the renewal day and the hard limit, each behind the second check (MA-76, MA-102), each an audit row and a message to the admins; a ceiling that takes prod and dev together past the plan's credit warns that the extra needs a card and needs an explicit yes, and one above the hard limit is refused with the steps to raise the Console limit first.
  • Caps and alerts (MA-137, ARCHITECTURE.md §14.2), in the same section: the Claude ladder's steps and guards; the AWS and others cap (£50), its steps (50%, 80%, 100%, forecast) and actions (dev pauses first; alerts and chat never stop), metered daily from Cost Explorer; the AWS Budgets backstop at £75 in Terraform; one alert router to the app's Alerts screen, push to the admin's devices and email through SNS (1,000 a month free), with quiet hours, cooldowns and a digest; "failed work" alerts from the dead-letter queues carry a Replay button (§19, MA-145).
  • The Admin center's admin actions in the api Lambda, behind the authorizer of phase 1 and each behind the second check (ARCHITECTURE.md §8.3; the security actions are the owner's alone, MA-102): the security actions (MA-102), the ceiling and caps with their alert routing (MA-116, MA-137) and replaying failed work (MA-145) in this phase; deciding agents' requests, rolling a setting back and closing an incident (MA-130, MA-135) join in phase 13; JSON limited to what that member may see.
  • Several admins, everyone else views (MA-308): the owner grants or takes back the admin role in the chat, with Confirm, the second check and an audit row; the owner is always an admin. Admins add, correct and delete; every other member views only, their own preferences aside, at every door and in every route.
  • The owner's security actions (MA-102): signing out or pausing any member's devices from the Admin center. A paused member's sign-in is refused with one line until the owner restores them, and the owner cannot pause their own account (your call, 29 Oct 2026).
  • More than one way in (MA-77): a member adds a second sign-in, Apple or Google, or a passkey, from You, with a fresh sign-in on the new account and no second check (your call, 29 Oct 2026); the owner can unlink one from the Admin center; every link, unlink and sign-in an audit row.
  • The judges, calibrated by the admin (MA-255, ARCHITECTURE.md §21.5): each month, and whenever a judge, a prompt, a tool or the model changes, the Admin center offers about 30 runs with their transcripts and verdicts; the admin marks pass or fail per failure mode with a one-line critique, behind the second check; labels accumulate, each keeping its run's transcript; a judge acts once it has 50 pass and 50 fail labels, and one below 0.8 on its true-positive or true-negative rate drops to advisory; a run marked failed becomes a regression case once rewritten onto the seed household and confirmed behind the second check; the eval caps are seen and moved here. Designed first with /design-first.
  • The web app's Audit, Files and Admin center (MA-184): the same pages in a browser, from the browser boards in MyPaisa app v1, with the second check a passkey or a fresh sign-in for the admins' actions.
  • Already done: the Warm ledger design system (MA-101) and the mark (MA-103).
