4.9 Commitments and check-ins

The agent asks; the member taps. Built in phase 4 (MA-205) and used by every kind after it, this replaces §8's one-off reminders (the first-of-the-month question and the 35-day nudge for a valuation).

  • What a check-in is. A Commitment (§4.1) is any expected, dated money tied to a record or a member: an EMI, a card bill, a SIP, rent, a premium, a salary, a maturity, a valuation due. Its kind's templates make it when the record is confirmed. A CheckIn is one occurrence, with the id commitment#due_date, created once by a conditional put, so a retry never asks twice.
  • The daily job. A worker job, code with no LLM, finds what is due and puts each check-in card straight on the outbox, with push (no amounts on the lock screen, MA-170):
HDFC car loan: was November's EMI paid?
[Paid] [Not yet] [Different amount] [Paid off in full]
  • Answers.
    • Paid: the card already shows exactly what will be written, so the tap is the Confirm, with Undo. One transaction moves the check-in to answered, writes the event and its audit row (via: checkin), and updates the commitment.
    • Not yet: asks again in a few days, then shows as overdue.
    • Different amount and Paid off in full open a short flow. Paid off in full closes the record, ends the commitment and runs the Projection again.
  • Several due at once become one card with All paid.
  • Joint records: the payer is asked, and the other owner is told.
  • Autopay commitments are assumed paid after their grace period unless a statement or the member says otherwise, and are listed in a weekly digest, which keeps to about ten notifications a week (§11).
  • A statement that shows the payment answers the check-in by itself (below).
  • The same machinery covers an FD's maturity (renewed, withdrawn or moved, §4.8), a card bill (in full, the minimum, or part), a SIP (went through, skipped or stopped), a salary (credited or changed) and a valuation due (a property every six months).

Statements (phase 6, MA-218). A bank or card statement (PDF, CSV or XLSX, from UK and Indian banks) sent in the chat is kept in S3 for good, like every file (§4.6), and then read:

  • each line is categorised, the merchant memory first, then Haiku for the merchants it has not seen;
  • transfers are recognised (a card payment, a move between the household's own accounts, an EMI matched to its commitment), so nothing counts twice; on a card only interest and fees are spending, and an EMI's principal is saving;
  • one review card follows, grouped by category with the uncertain lines in amber, and one Confirm;
  • a payment it shows answers its check-in.

A statement is routine, so it takes no second check (§4.5); the Excel history importer keeps its own (§18.5).

The month closes on the 1st (phase 6, MA-220). A check-in shows last month's plan against actual per category, asks only for what no statement covered (cash, a bank with no statement), and closes the month with one Confirm. The Budget screen reads the result (§12).

4.10 Writing: create in the chat, correct in place

Rule 4 (§1), built in phase 2 (MA-201):

  • Creating anything (a record, an event, a kind, a commitment, a plan item) happens only in the chat: a card, a Confirm tap, an audit row. A check-in's tap is the Confirm of an exact card (§4.9).
  • Correcting an existing figure works in place on any screen, at either door: a value, a rate, a date, an amount or an assumption.
    1. Tap the figure and type the new value.
    2. A before → after line appears, with Save.
    3. Save writes the change as a correction event with its audit row (via: inline_edit).
  • Nothing can be created in place, and a derived figure (an EMI, a maturity value, net worth) is not editable: its inputs are.
  • One write service takes both paths. It validates the change against the kind spec (the field exists, is editable, and has the right type and range; or, for the admin's PlanItem and ProjectionAssumptions changes, against their schema, §6.6), writes the event and its audit row in one transaction (§4.6), and the views move. Save carries the figure the screen showed and is refused if the record has moved since, so two doors never overwrite each other. A confirmed change reaches every open door on the stream as records.changed (§21.5).
  • The api's correction route is PATCH /v1/records/{id}, for the spec's editable fields (and the admin's plan items and saved Projection assumptions, §6.6) only; every other data route is GET.
  • Who may change what:
    • a member corrects their own records' figures;
    • the owner corrects anyone's;
    • plan items, a rearrangement's Confirm and saved Projection assumptions are the admin's alone (§4.5, §6.6).
  • The Admin center stays the one other exception: the owner's admin settings and operations, never a record, each behind the second check (§9.3).
