4.9 Commitments and check-ins

The agent asks; the member taps. Built in phase 4 (MA-205) and used by every kind after it, this replaces §7's one-off reminders (the first-of-the-month question and the 35-day nudge for a valuation).

  • What a check-in is. A Commitment (§4.1) is any expected, dated money tied to a record or a member: an EMI, a card bill, a SIP, rent, a premium, a salary, a maturity, a valuation due. Its kind's templates make it when the record is confirmed. A CheckIn is one occurrence, with the id commitment#due_date, created once by a conditional put, so a retry never asks twice.
  • The daily job. A worker job, code with no LLM, finds what is due and puts each check-in card straight on the outbox, with push (no amounts on the lock screen, MA-170):
HDFC home loan: was November's EMI paid?
[Paid] [Not yet] [Different amount] [Paid off in full]
  • Answers.
    • Paid: the card already shows exactly what will be written, so the tap is the Confirm, with Undo. One transaction moves the check-in to answered, writes the event and its audit row (via: checkin), and updates the commitment.
    • Not yet: asks again in a few days, then shows as overdue.
    • Different amount and Paid off in full open a short flow. Paid off in full closes the record, ends the commitment and runs the Projection again.
  • Several due at once become one card with All paid.
  • Who is asked: a check-in's tap writes a record, so check-ins are sent to the admins and answered by admins only (§1, rule 4). A member who is not an admin may see that a check-in is pending, on the record and in what's due, but gets no Paid, Not yet, Different amount or Paid off in full buttons. On a joint record the card names the payer.
  • Autopay commitments are assumed paid after their grace period unless a statement or an admin says otherwise, and are listed in a weekly digest, which keeps to about ten notifications a week (§10).
  • A statement that shows the payment answers the check-in by itself (below).
  • The same machinery covers an FD's maturity (renewed, withdrawn or moved, §4.8), a card bill (in full, the minimum, or part), a SIP (went through, skipped or stopped), a salary (credited or changed) and a valuation due (a property every six months).

Statements (phase 6, MA-218). A bank or card statement (PDF, CSV or XLSX, from UK and Indian banks) sent in the chat is kept in S3 for good, like every file (§4.6), and then read:

  • each line is categorised, the merchant memory first, then Haiku for the merchants it has not seen;
  • transfers are recognised (a card payment, a move between the household's own accounts, an EMI matched to its commitment), so nothing counts twice; on a card only interest and fees are spending, and an EMI's principal is saving;
  • one review card follows, grouped by category with the uncertain lines in amber, and one Confirm;
  • a payment it shows answers its check-in.

A statement is routine, so it takes no second check (§4.5); the Excel history importer keeps its own (§17.5).

The month closes on the 1st (phase 6, MA-220). A check-in shows last month's plan against actual per category, asks only for what no statement covered (cash, a bank with no statement), and closes the month with one Confirm. The Budget screen reads the result (§11).

4.10 Writing: admins only; add and delete in the chat, correct in the chat or in place

Rule 4 (§1), built in phase 2 (MA-201, MA-309), with the roles of phase 8 (MA-308):

  • Adding anything (a record, an event, a kind, a commitment, a plan item) happens only in the chat: a card, a Confirm tap, an audit row. A check-in's tap is the Confirm of an exact card (§4.9).
  • Correcting an existing figure (a value, a rate, a date, an amount or an assumption) works in the chat ("the SBI FD's rate is 7.25%": a card with the before and after, Confirm) or in place on any screen, at either door:
    1. Tap the figure and type the new value.
    2. A before → after line appears, with Save.
    3. Save writes the change as a correction event with its audit row (via: inline_edit).
  • Deleting a record, an event or a plan item happens only in the chat (MA-309): "delete the old Monzo pot" drafts the delete, and a card names what goes with it (child records, commitments, open check-ins); Confirm, then the second check (§4.5). The record's status becomes deleted: it is hidden from every view, total and Projection run, and its events, audit rows and files are kept for good. "Restore the Monzo pot" brings it back the same way, with its audit row. Deleting is for a record added by mistake; closing one that ended (closed, matured, sold) stays separate. Nothing is ever erased.
  • Nothing can be added or deleted in place, and a derived figure (an EMI, a maturity value, net worth) is not editable: its inputs are.
  • One write service takes every path. It validates the change against the kind spec (the field exists, is editable, and has the right type and range; or, for the admin's PlanItem and ProjectionAssumptions changes, against their schema, §6), writes the event and its audit row in one transaction (§4.6), and the views move. Save carries the figure the screen showed and is refused if the record has moved since, so two doors never overwrite each other. A confirmed change reaches every open door on the stream as records.changed (§19.5).
  • The api's correction route is PATCH /v1/records/{id}, only from an admin, for the spec's editable fields (and plan items and saved Projection assumptions, §6) only; every other data route is GET. Adding and deleting have no route: they go through the chat.
  • Who may change what: only an admin adds, corrects or deletes, on any member's records, and alone saves plan items, a rearrangement's Confirm and the Projection's assumptions (§4.5, §6). The owner grants or takes back the admin role in the chat (MA-308). Every other member views only, their own preferences aside; every write tool and route refuses them, in code.
  • The Admin center stays the one other exception: admin settings and operations, never a record, each behind the second check (§8.3).
