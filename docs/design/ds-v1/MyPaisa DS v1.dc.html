It is the design system in force: every MyPaisa screen is designed in it from now on. 1b and 1c stay below as the record of what was considered. Since MA-301 (Arun, 2 Nov 2026): a card is one surface, its rows and figures split by hairlines, under short sentence-case labels (d 1a); each door uses its platform’s own type and icons, SF Symbols in the app and Lucide on the web (f 1a); and the chat sits beside every screen from about 1393 pt, a sheet away from about 600 to 1393 pt, and a tab in a compact window (every iPhone, a folded foldable, Slide Over), on the web a drawer below 1393 px (c 1c). Foundations, Components and the iOS kit carry the change; the frames below keep the stand-in icons they were drawn with.

Arun asked for a new design system after the Monzo app and Apple's Human Interface Guidelines, for the iOS app and the web app (MA-101). Each direction is shown on the Overview at phone width (light, dark) and in the browser (light, dark), and on one in-app chat card. Figures, members and holdings are illustrative. Inspired by Monzo, never its name, logo or assets. 1a is worked out in full: Foundations, Components and the iOS kit here, and every screen in MyPaisa iOS v1 and MyPaisa app v1.

Why 1a

The summary first, in one glance. The navy hero card is Monzo's account card made ours: one number, one plain line, one chart. "Nothing to do this week" sits right under it. Calm about losses.
