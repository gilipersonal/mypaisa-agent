ITelegram kit: the PNG images

The images MyPaisa renders and sends are the design system's real presence in the chat: everything else in a Telegram message is Telegram's. Three templates, each a 324 × 216 layout exported at 1080 × 720 and shown here at half the export size. Navy is the default; light is the same template on paper. Illustrative figures.

Why navy is the default

  • A bot cannot see the member's Telegram theme. The hero navy reads on Telegram light and dark alike, so every image ships navy unless the member picks light in Settings.
  • It is the Mini App's hero card, so the chat and the dashboard feel like one thing.

One house style

  • Coral line, area under it at 18%, three gridlines, GBP axis on the right, months under the plot at mid-month.
  • Stances sit in a lane above the plot, each a pill with its word and a dotted leader to its dot, so colour is never the only signal. Every label is real text.
  • Coral is the price line only. Holdings use chart-2, chart-5, chart-4, a neutral and the “other” grey: never green (gains only), never coral (alarm).
  • Footer on every image: the coin, then a legend or the price date.

Sizes and limits

  • 1080 × 720 for all three, so they sit together in an album without cropping. Telegram shows them about 306 to 310 wide in a phone bubble and about 394 on Telegram Desktop.
  • On a phone the overline and chips read at 12 or more, the axis at 11, the stance pills at 10.
  • The numbers come from the engine; the renderer draws, it never computes.
