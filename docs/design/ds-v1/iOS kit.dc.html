Two layers. Content is solid Warm ledger: the navy hero, cards, grouped lists, charts and messages. Liquid Glass is only the layer of controls that floats above it, and content scrolls under it.

Where glass goes. The tab bar, the sidebar, toolbar buttons, the composer and its chips, menus, partial sheets and notification platters. Glass carries labels only, in ink or ink-2; over dark content iOS turns it dark.

Tint with intent. Only the primary control of a group is tinted coral: Send. Confirm on a card is a filled content button, never glass.

Reduce Transparency. The system makes glass opaque, and the app's own glass follows: the surface colour, no blur (.rt on the boards).

Built with the system bars and .glassEffect() in a GlassEffectContainer, so a group morphs as one: the shell in MA-163, the composer in MA-169.

Dynamic Type, not fixed sizes. The DS's scale is Apple's at the Large setting, so each class is one text style and follows the member's setting from xSmall to AX5.

Hero figures use SF Pro Rounded, heavy, scaled with the large title. Every figure uses tabular numerals, so columns line up and a counting number never jitters.

iPhone: one glass tab bar. Chat · Home · Activity · You, with Search as iOS 26's separate search tab. The selected tab sits in a lens, in the accent, its symbol filled.

Minimised on scroll. As the conversation scrolls, the bar shrinks to its selected tab and the composer takes the bottom; scrolling back or a tap brings it back.

Search takes the bar when it opens, and finds a holding, a change, a file or a past answer (MA-180).

iPad and an open foldable: the sidebar. The same sections in a glass sidebar (.sidebarAdaptable), with Owner apart. The Admin center's red badge counts only what asks for action; members never see Owner.

Motion. The selected tab's lens glides to the tapped tab (glassEffectID) and the bar shrinks with .smooth as content scrolls; the sidebar stays put while content moves.

44 pt rows and tabs, labels never truncated at Large. The shell is MA-163; the chat tab is MA-169.

Content buttons. Primary (filled accent) for the one action that matters, tinted for the others, ghost for Cancel, alert red only for a destructive act. Never glass; at least 44 pt tall.

Pressed and disabled. Pressed scales to 97% and darkens a step (lightens in dark); glass brightens and swells. Disabled is 40% and takes no tap, with the reason said nearby.

Chips. Shortcut chips are glass, they live with the composer, and their tap area is 44 pt. Stance chips are content: always a word, and on a holding's own card its confidence and "a view, not advice".

Motion. Every control answers the finger with .snappy(extraBounce: 0.08): buttons dip to 95%, the segmented lens and the switch knob glide, a chip ticks with a selection haptic.

No Buy or Sell. MyPaisa cannot trade, so no button anywhere offers to. The segmented control and switch are the system's, coloured by the tokens. MA-165 builds them in MyPaisaUI.

One house style in Swift Charts. The chat's cards and the dashboard draw the same charts from the engine's series; no chart is an image any more (MA-56, MA-58).

Lines and areas are marks; words are text. Labels, values and the stance's word are SwiftUI text over the plot, as tokens.css's chart rule says: never a value baked into a drawing.
