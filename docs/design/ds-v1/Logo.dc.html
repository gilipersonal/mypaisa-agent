1MyPaisa logo: three animated marks

MA-103. Arun asked for a motion-animated brand. Three different ideas, each drawn by hand on a 48 grid in DS v1 Warm ledger: the hero navy (#13213A, #1A2944 on dark), coral (#FF6B4F, #FF7A5E on dark), ink, white and the warm ground #F6F4F1, with the hero card's own inner tones where a coin sits on navy. Each option shows the mark from 512 to 16 px in light and dark, the app icon and Telegram's circle crop, the lockups with a clear-space rule, its intro (one play, at most 1.2 s, with Replay and a six-frame storyboard), and the mark at work: the Mini App opening inside Telegram, the sign-in page, Telegram's chat list and chat header, and a browser tab. Under Reduce Motion every intro shows its last frame at once. Figures are illustrative.

check_circlePicked: 1c Watcher (Arun, 24 Oct 2026). It is now the MyPaisa mark: the repo's docs/mark.svg family and every Claude Design screen use it (the sidebar, the sign-in pages, the bot's avatar and the bot's PNG footers). 1a and 1b stay below as the record. The record, as written before the pick: this page recommended 1a Stance line, because it is the one mark here that could not belong to another app. It is made of things MyPaisa already shows: the navy hero card, the coral line on every chart and every PNG the bot sends, and the ringed stance dot. So the brand and the product say the same thing, and the intro is the product in one second: a line drawn calmly, then one view placed. It holds at 16 px with its own small cut, and it needs no £, so it stays right if the household ever holds more than sterling. 1c is the safe evolution of today's coin; 1b is the plainest and the sharpest at 16 px, but the hardest to own.
