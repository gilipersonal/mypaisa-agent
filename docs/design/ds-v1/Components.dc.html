The building blocks of direction 1a, "Warm ledger", each in light and dark from one markup, with its anatomy and its states. Light sits above dark so every specimen keeps its real phone width (358). Every class here is in tokens.css; nothing on this page adds a colour. This page holds the controls and content blocks; Components 2 holds navigation, overlays, list states and charts. Illustrative figures; members are "You" and "Asha".

Four buttons, four states. In the app and the web app alike a button navigates, opens the chat, or answers a card the chat drew (Confirm, Edit, Cancel): records are created in the chat and corrected in place, and MyPaisa never trades, so there is no Buy or Sell to design.

2Chips

A stance is a view, not advice, and the chip says which one in a word. Six stance chips, a neutral chip for plain facts, and the alert chip for the red tier only. The bot cannot trade, so no chip ever sits beside a Buy or Sell.

AnatomyA pill: padding 4 10, 13/18 semibold, +1% tracking, one line, an optional 18 icon 4 before the word. .chip-s (padding 2 8, 12/16) sits inside list rows beside the sub-line.
ContrastText on its own tint: ADD 4.6:1, HOLD 5.6:1, PAUSE 5.5:1, TRIM 5.0:1, REBALANCE 5.8:1, WATCH 6.4:1 in light; 6.6:1 or more in dark. Neutral 6.5:1 and 7.5:1; alert 5.4:1 and 6.2:1.
The word is the signalAlways the stance word, then the confidence: "PAUSE 61%". Colour only supports it, so the chip reads the same in greyscale and to a screen reader.
NeutralFor facts: the wrapper (ISA, GIA), the price date, audited facts. Never for a stance.
AlertOnly the red tier, which asks you to act, and it always says why in words. An everyday fall is quiet ink with ▼, never this chip.
Icons inside chipsInherit the chip's text colour. Never put a stance class on a bare icon: it brings its tint with it. Use .c-pause and friends for text-only colour.

SegmentedTrack --mp-surface-2, radius 10, padding 2, gap 2. Segments are 40 high (44 with the track), radius 8, 13/18 semibold. Selected: --mp-seg-on (white in light, a lifted #3A4456 in dark) with ink, 17.9:1 and 9.0:1, and a 1 px shadow. Others: ink-2, 6.5:1 and 7.5:1.

BehaviourTwo to four short segments, one or two words each. Each is a button with aria-pressed; the content below swaps in place and the page does not move.
Switch51 × 31, knob 27, on is --mp-up; the knob slides 20 over 200 ms. role="switch" with aria-checked; the row's label names the setting and the whole row toggles it.
Not colour aloneOff, the track carries a 1.5 px ring in ink-3: 5.4:1 light and 5.7:1 dark against the surface. On, the green track is 5.4:1 and 8.7:1 against the surface. The knob's side carries the state too.
View switchesA view switch here never writes your records. Records are created in the chat and corrected in place, or through a card and a Confirm tap in the chat.

Holding rowLead tile 36, radius 10, neutral, two letters. Name in Headline; under it a small stance chip and a short sub-line in Footnote ink-2 (MyMap · ISA, not the full fund name). Value in Headline, right-aligned and tabular; the week's change under it, ▲ in --mp-up or ▼ in --mp-down-quiet.
The row boxAt least 44 high, padding 11 16 (10 for two lines), gap 12. Hairlines in --mp-line, half a pixel, inset 16 from the left. The group has radius 14 and no shadow.
Section headerOverline in ink-3, inset 16, 6 above the group; a Footnote on the right names the column (week, value).
Setting rowA 32 icon tile (.mp-icontile, radius 9, neutral), the label in Body, then a value in ink-2, a switch, or a chevron. Settings that write say where they change: in the chat.
Row that opens a pageEnds in chevron_right 18 in ink-3; the whole row is the link. Never a chevron on a row that does nothing.
PressedThe row fills with --mp-surface-2 while it is held and the next page pushes in over 350 ms. Long names clip with an ellipsis; figures never wrap.

This week.mp-card, padding 14 16, gap 12: a 26 filled icon, the sentence in Headline, the next step in Footnote ink-2, and a chevron to the detail. The whole card is the link.
Tone by icon and wordsAll clear: check_circle in --mp-up. Worth a look: warning in .c-pause. Asks for action: warning in .c-alert. The sentence always says which; colour is never the only signal.
Red is rareOnly when you set an alert and it fired, or something needs your decision. A normal fall, a PAUSE or late prices are never red.
Stat card.mp-card, radius 20, padding 16, --mp-shadow. Overline in ink-3, one figure in .mp-num-l 28/32 rounded on one line, a Footnote saying what it means. Two across at every size; on the browser they sit in the narrow column. Cards in a row share a height.
FiguresEvery figure comes from the engine with its date. A stat card never shows a number the AI wrote.
BrowserOn wide screens the summary card can carry a small tinted Open chat button instead of the chevron, since the chat is a separate window there.
7Privacy mask

The mask.mp-mask: a --mp-surface-2 pill (--mp-hero-2 on the hero), radius 8, at least 64 wide, the height of the line it replaces. Text inside is transparent.
What hides, what staysPounds hide: values, totals, amounts in sentences. Percentages, ▲ ▼, stance chips, confidence and chart shapes stay.
Never in the pageThe mask holds a placeholder with the same number of digits (£18,640 becomes £00,000), never the real figure, and carries aria-label="Hidden", so a screen reader or a screenshot cannot leak it.
Nothing movesFigures are tabular, so the placeholder is exactly as wide as the figure it hides: names keep the same room and clip in the same place. Figures narrower than 64 get the 64 minimum.
The switchThe eye button: visibility when pounds show, visibility_off when hidden, labelled "Privacy mode". It sits next to the whose-view control on every page.
ChartsCharts keep their line and shape; any pound labels on them are masked like the rest.
