Tab statesSelected: --mp-accent, the icon in it too, aria-current="page"; Lucide has no filled forms, so the colour marks it (the app’s SF Symbols fill). Others: ink-3, outlined (5.3:1 and 5.9:1 on the material). Focus: the 3 px ring.
Sidebar248 wide including its padding (20 14); the coin and name on top, six items, who is signed in at the foot. Items 44 high, radius 10, 15/20 medium in ink-2; selected lifts onto the surface with --mp-shadow, semibold ink, accent icon.
Which oneThe tab bar in compact and regular, the sidebar in wide (1024 and over); .mp-shell swaps them from one markup. The labels are the same words in both; only More differs: on the tab bar it holds what the sidebar lists, Activity and Audit.
Where the chat sitsThe chat sits beside every screen from about 1393 pt, a sheet away from about 600 to 1393 pt, and a tab in a compact window (every iPhone, a folded foldable, Slide Over). On the web: a column beside the page from 1393 px; from 1024 to 1393 px a drawer over the page, opened from the sidebar’s Chat (the iPad’s sheet, on the web); under 1024 px, where the tab bar stays, the Chat tab (MA-301 c 1c, Arun, 2 Nov 2026).
IconsLucide, inline SVG in .lc (22, a 2 px stroke in currentColor): house, wallet, chart-pie, trending-up, history, clipboard-check and menu here. In the app the same places use SF Symbols (MA-301 f 1a).
Pushing a pageA row or card that opens a page pushes it in from the right over 350 ms on --mp-ease-sheet; Back pops it. Tabs switch without motion.

Sheet.mp-sheet: surface, top corners 16, padding 8 16 24, shadow 0 −8 40 at 18%. A 36 × 5 grabber; a title in Title 3 with a plain Done on the right, so the keyboard and VoiceOver can close it too.
Order insideSummary first: the figure and what it covers, the plain sentence, "What it counts", the figure beside it. Technical detail is one tap away; Ask in the chat is the way on.
Motion and scrimRises over 420 ms on --mp-ease-sheet (.mp-sheet-in); the page behind dims under .mp-scrim, a deep navy-black at 42%, covering the page behind the sheet. Drag down, tap the scrim or tap Done to close.
Toast.mp-toast: inverse, ink fill with background-colour text (16.3:1 and 17.6:1), radius 14, 15/20 medium, an 18 icon. One line where it can, two at most.
Toast behaviourSits 16 above the tab bar (or bottom-centre on the browser), fades in over 200 ms, leaves after 4 seconds, role="status". Never the only place something important is said.
Not for changesNeither confirms a change to your money: those are cards you confirm in the chat, and the chat replies.

11Charts and tracks

Charts tell one thing each and carry their number above them, not on an axis. One line in the accent, one quiet area, a hairline grid; status colours only where an event is marked; fixed colours for currencies; category hues for mixes, always named in a legend. The SVG only draws lines and areas; every dot, label and axis tick is HTML on top (.mp-chartbox). Meters and progress sit on .mp-track.

The chart box.mp-chartbox holds an SVG on a 0 to 100 viewBox with preserveAspectRatio="none", so it stretches with its card while vector-effect: non-scaling-stroke keeps the line at 2.5 px. Dots (.mp-chartdot), labels (.mp-chartlbl) and ticks (.mp-chartaxis) are HTML placed by % on top. Never a template value inside SVG text: it does not render.
Line and area--mp-chart-line on surfaces (coral over --mp-hero-area on the hero); --mp-chart-area under it; three grid lines at most in --mp-chart-grid; months in ink-3, mid-month. The figure above carries the number, so there is no y-axis on the phone. The chart fades in over 200 ms (.mp-fade): .mp-draw works only on a path that scales with its viewBox, so it breaks under a non-scaling stroke.
Event markersOn the day each event happened (a bonus, a buy, a big one-off): a surface dot with a 2.5 px ring in its status colour, and the word beside it in the same colour (4.6:1 or more). Labels move above, below or to the upper left so they never sit on the line.
Threshold lineA level you set (a cash floor, a budget plan) is 1 px, dashed 4 4, ink-3, labelled with its level in words in ink-2; it marks your threshold and is never red. A forecast (the Projection, the Budget forecast) is drawn as its own chart, never as a tail on a price line.
Bars and tracksBars are 12 high on .mp-track, whose inset ring keeps the empty part visible. Currencies always use --mp-ccy-*; meters and progress use --mp-meter, with role="meter" or progressbar. Values at the end, tabular. Grow in over 700 ms.
CategoriesFixed encodings first: an asset mix always uses --mp-asset-* (shares blue, bonds purple, silver grey, cash amber), currencies --mp-ccy-*. Never --mp-chart-3 or coral for a category: green means gains and coral is the line. 2 px gaps between segments, every segment named in a legend with its value, and a text alternative that states the point.
