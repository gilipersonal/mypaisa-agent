0Telegram kit: the rules

The Telegram side of MyPaisa DS v1, direction 1a Warm ledger (MA-101). Every message the bot sends, each in Telegram light and dark. Telegram draws the chat; MyPaisa owns the words, their order, which buttons appear, and the PNG images, which are where the design system reaches the chat. The guided flow, keyboards, command menu and glossary are in Telegram kit controls; the PNG templates at export size in Telegram kit images. Illustrative figures; members are You and Asha.

Telegram draws, MyPaisa owns

Telegram draws

  • Bubbles, tails, times, read ticks, the wallpaper
  • Inline buttons, the reply keyboard, the command menu, the alert box for a glossary tap
  • Light or dark, from the member's own Telegram theme. The bot never sees which.

MyPaisa owns

  • The words and their order
  • Which buttons appear, and their labels
  • The PNG images: charts and the summary tile in the hero navy with the coral line. Navy reads on both themes, so it is the default.
  • The bot's photo: the coin

Button labels

  • Actions are verbs: Confirm, Edit, Cancel, I acted, Undo.
  • Two words at most. Places end with a right chevron (Balanced ▸). Folds end with a down chevron when shut, up when open. Questions keep their mark (Why?).
  • No emoji, ever. Chevrons, ▲ and ▼ are text.
  • At most three buttons a row and four rows a card; about 10 characters at 3-up, 16 at 2-up.
  • One web_app button a card, full width, last: Open dashboard.

Message length

  • Simple mode fits one phone screen above the keyboard: the image, about eight lines, the buttons.
  • The layman's line first. Detail waits behind Technical or Why?
  • Telegram's limits: a photo caption 1,024 characters, a message 4,096, a glossary alert 200.
  • Technical mode opens every fold (/technical).
  • A guided flow edits one message in place. It never stacks.

Buttons never trade

  • No Buy, Sell or Execute anywhere. MyPaisa cannot trade.
  • I acted records what you did elsewhere. Confirm saves a record you have read.
  • Every stance says "a view, not advice".
  • Figures come from the engine. The words never say Claude worked them out.
  • Text has no colour in Telegram: gains, falls and unsure fields are carried by ▲ ▼, words and underline. Colour lives in the PNGs.
