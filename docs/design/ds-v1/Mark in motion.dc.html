DS v1The mark in motion

How MyPaisa’s mark, 1c Watcher, moves. Arun picked 1a, Watcher at work, on 25 Nov 2026 (MA-355, item d): the mark is still by default, arrives once when the app opens, and after that moves only when MyPaisa does something for you: one lap of the dot when it starts on your message, and one ring when a change is saved. The coin stays put; only the dot travels.

This board is the spec for the web app, where the mark sits at the top left of the sidebar, 32 px beside the name. The iPhone and iPad are on iPhone v4 · Mark in motion. Every stage is the real animation; Play again runs it, and the strips are the same animation paused at the times shown.

What it supersedes. It extends Logo’s record of MA-103’s intro for 1c (its “4 · Motion” stages and “App opening” frames), which stay as the record of the pick. The arrival here is that intro carried to the small cut: the pound now writes itself where it faded in, and the dot travels on --mo-travel where it used --mp-ease. The lap and the ring are new.

What motion.css gained (MA-355). One easing, --mo-travel: the .smooth spring itself, sampled with linear(), for a part that travels far and must leave from rest (--mo-smooth is only that spring’s landing, and starts a full lap at full speed). And the mark’s keyframes, mo-logo-*. Everything else is an existing token.

When it stays still

Privacy mode has no effect. The mark holds no figure, so it looks and moves the same with amounts hidden.

The lockup is not a control. The mark and the name are not a link: no hover state, no pointer cursor, no place in the Tab order, and nothing plays when the pointer or the keyboard reaches them.

Only the sidebar’s mark moves on the web. The marks beside MyPaisa’s messages in the chat stay still, so one mark moves at a time. Under 1024 px there is no sidebar, so on the phone’s browser nothing moves: the marks in its chat stay still too.

Nothing else starts it. Not a timer, not a page change, not a figure rolling, not a notification. A reply that streams for a long time gets one lap, at its start.

Reduce Motion. The system setting swaps each moment for its still version above; the board plays that version here wherever the system asks for it.

Under the dim. The desktop chat is a sheet now (MA-355 a 1a), and its dim covers the whole shell, the sidebar with it. So the lap, and a ring for a save confirmed in the chat, play under the dim: quieter than on the open page, still seen. The arrival, and a ring for a figure saved in place on a page, play on the open page.

The page. Home’s head is the four pills of MA-355 b 1a; there is no chat column. The cards under the hero are today’s, to give the mark a page to sit beside; MA-355 c redraws them.

Build. web/src/lib/components/brand/Mark.svelte gains its moving parts (the bite as a mask that travels with the dot) and play(open | lap | saved); AppShell.svelte calls it on mount, when a turn starts and when a record is saved, and keeps the one-at-a-time rule; the scene rules sit in shell.css beside .mpm; the keyframes and --mo-travel are in motion.css.
