MyPaisa DS v1 · Foundations

Direction 1a, "Warm ledger": Monzo's warmth on the bones of Apple's Human Interface Guidelines. Every value on this page comes from tokens.css; every specimen is drawn twice from one markup, light (.t-light) and dark (.t-dark), the way the app follows the device's colour scheme. Contrast ratios are measured with the WCAG 2.2 formula: text needs 4.5:1, graphics and focus rings 3:1. Illustrative figures throughout; members are "You" and "Asha". Updated for MA-301 (Arun, 2 Nov 2026): a card is one surface, its rows and figures split by hairlines, under short sentence-case labels (d 1a); each door uses its platform’s own type and icons, Lucide on the web (f 1a); and the chat sits beside the page only from 1393 (c 1c).

1Colour

Semantic tokens, never raw hex in a screen. Each row shows the token in light and dark with the surface it is used on: text tokens are drawn as "Aa" on that surface, fills carry the text that sits on them, lines and marks are drawn as strokes. The ratio is the pair that matters for that token. The fixed encodings at the end keep one meaning on every page. Green means good, and only that: gains with ▲ and status-OK marks (done, verified, nothing to do). It is never a category, a progress bar or a meter.

2Type

Apple's Dynamic Type sizes at the default "Large" setting, in the system font, so the app and phone web read like part of the phone. Figures that carry the story (the household total, a headline stat) are set rounded and heavy. One size per job; hierarchy comes from size and weight, never from colour alone. Each door uses its platform’s own type and icons (MA-301 f 1a, Arun, 2 Nov 2026): nothing is loaded from Google Fonts and there is no icon font in the build.

Text: SF Pro in the app; on the web the system font, then Inter-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "SF Pro Text", "Inter", system-ui, sans-serif. On the web Inter is self-hosted, one variable WOFF2 (Latin), preloaded; a Mac, an iPhone or an iPad draws SF Pro first.
Figures: SF Pro Rounded; on the web a Nunito figures subsetui-rounded, "SF Pro Rounded", "Nunito", -apple-system, system-ui. On the web Nunito 800 is a small self-hosted subset: the digits, £, ₹ and their separators. Only .mp-num-hero and .mp-num-l.
Tabular figures everywhere.mp sets font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums, so pounds and percentages line up in columns and do not jitter when they count up.
Labels: short, sentence case.mp-over is 13/18 semibold with no tracking, never all capitals: “Net worth · Mon 26 Oct”, “Top holdings”, “Coming up”. It fits one line at 390; a longer one is shortened, not wrapped (MA-301 d 1a).
Icons: SF Symbols in the app, Lucide on the webInline SVG on the web (.lc), so an icon never shows as a word when a font fails to load. See 6 · Iconography.
On these boardsThe boards still load Inter, Nunito and Material Symbols from Google Fonts while they draw .ms stand-ins; the web app’s own tokens.css has no @import, and the boards’ goes with the last .ms (MA-340).

3Space, shape, elevation

A 4 pt grid, six radii that grow with the size of the thing, and three heights of surface. Grouped lists sit flat on the background like iOS Settings; cards lift a little; only sheets cast a real shadow. A card is one surface: inside it nothing is boxed again, and its rows and figures are split by hairlines (.mp-rows, .mp-hairs; MA-301 d 1a). Every tappable thing is at least 44 × 44 pt, even when the glyph inside is 22.

4Layout and size classes

One markup, three size classes. The app's root, .mp-app, is a size container, so the frame decides, not the device: the same page is one column on a phone, two columns in an iPad or narrow browser window, and a sidebar layout in a laptop browser. The widths are Apple's size classes in web pixels. The chat joins the page as a column only from 1393; below that it is a drawer on the web from 1024 (the Chat tab under 1024, where the tab bar stays) and a sheet on the iPad. Light and dark are stacked here so the wide frames have room.

The frame decides, not the device.mp-app is container: app / inline-size. A narrow laptop browser, an iPad in Split View and a tablet browser switch class at the same widths as a browser, and again when they are resized.
One markup, no device builds.mp-shell holds .mp-shell-side (wide only) and .mp-shell-tabs (compact and regular). .mp-only-compact and .mp-only-wide show or hide the odd piece.
Content never stretchesRegular stays one column until 760, then splits into two equal columns. On wide the columns are minmax(0, 1.3fr) minmax(300px, 1fr), so the list never gets narrower than 300, and .mp-pagepad caps the content at 1180, centred past 1360. The stats are 2 × 2 at every size.
Where the chat sitsThe chat sits beside every screen from about 1393 pt, a sheet away from about 600 to 1393 pt, and a tab in a compact window (every iPhone, a folded foldable, Slide Over). On the web the unit is px: from 1393 px a column beside the page; from 1024 to 1393 px a drawer over the page, opened from the sidebar’s Chat (the iPad’s sheet, on the web); under 1024 px, where the tab bar stays, the Chat tab. No iPad is 1393 pt wide (the 13-inch is 1376 pt across), so on every iPad the chat is a sheet (MA-301 c 1c, Arun, 2 Nov 2026).

5Motion

Motion explains where something came from and then gets out of the way. Two curves and six durations cover every case.

Transform and opacity onlyNothing animates width, height, colour or layout; the browser keeps every frame on the compositor.
Nothing loopsEach animation plays once when its content arrives. The loading skeleton pulses four times, then rests.
Reduced motion lands at onceEvery animation sits inside prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference; with reduced motion the final state simply appears.

6Iconography

Each door draws its platform’s own icons (MA-301 f 1a, Arun, 2 Nov 2026). In the app, SF Symbols, weighted to the text beside them, filled for the selected tab and for status ticks. On the web, Lucide as inline SVG on a 24 grid, <svg class="lc">: a 2 px stroke with round caps and joins in currentColor, tree-shaken from @lucide/svelte (ISC licence), so an icon can never show as a word when a font fails to load. Each icon below is drawn in Lucide, its SF Symbol named under it. Nineteen icons cover the product; an icon always sits beside a word or carries an aria-label. No emoji anywhere; ▲ and ▼ are text glyphs for change, not icons. Boards not yet redrawn keep Material Symbols (.ms) as stand-ins.

7Voice

Plain, warm, second person: a friend who is good with money, not a terminal. Say what it means for you first and keep the detail one tap away. Figures come from the ledger and Friday's prices, never from the AI. MyPaisa never trades and never advises: it keeps the household's own records, budget and forecasts.
