Direction 1a, "Warm ledger": Monzo's warmth on the bones of Apple's Human Interface Guidelines. Every value on this page comes from tokens.css; every specimen is drawn twice from one markup, light (.t-light) and dark (.t-dark), the way the Mini App follows Telegram's colour scheme. Contrast ratios are measured with the WCAG 2.2 formula: text needs 4.5:1, graphics and focus rings 3:1. Illustrative figures throughout; members are "You" and "Asha".

1Colour

Semantic tokens, never raw hex in a screen. Each row shows the token in light and dark with the surface it is used on: text tokens are drawn as "Aa" on that surface, fills carry the text that sits on them, lines and marks are drawn as strokes. The ratio is the pair that matters for that token. The fixed encodings at the end keep one meaning on every page. Green means good, and only that: gains with ▲ and status-OK marks (done, verified, nothing to do). It is never a category, a progress bar or a meter.

2Type

Apple's Dynamic Type sizes at the default "Large" setting, in the system font, so the Mini App reads like part of the phone. Figures that carry the story (the household total, a headline stat) are set rounded and heavy. One size per job; hierarchy comes from size and weight, never from colour alone.

Text: SF Pro, Inter elsewhere-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "SF Pro Text", "Inter", system-ui, sans-serif

Tabular figures everywhere.mp sets font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums, so pounds and percentages line up in columns and do not jitter when they count up.

4Layout and size classes

One markup, three size classes. The app's root, .mp-app, is a size container, so the frame decides, not the device: the same page is one column on a phone, two columns in an iPad or Telegram Desktop window, and a sidebar layout in a laptop browser. The widths are Apple's size classes in web pixels. Light and dark are stacked here so the wide frames have room.

The frame decides, not the device.mp-app is container: app / inline-size. Telegram Desktop's Mini App window, an iPad in Split View and a tablet Mini App switch class at the same widths as a browser, and again when they are resized.
One markup, no device builds.mp-shell holds .mp-shell-side (wide only) and .mp-shell-tabs (compact and regular). .mp-only-compact and .mp-only-wide show or hide the odd piece.

5Motion

Motion explains where something came from and then gets out of the way. Two curves and six durations cover every case.

Transform and opacity onlyNothing animates width, height, colour or layout; the browser keeps every frame on the compositor.
Nothing loopsEach animation plays once when its content arrives. The loading skeleton pulses four times, then rests.
Reduced motion lands at onceEvery animation sits inside prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference; with reduced motion the final state simply appears.

6Iconography

Material Symbols Rounded stands in for SF Symbols on the web: weight 500, optical size 24, outlined by default and filled for the selected tab and for status ticks. Use the ligature name, <span class="ms">home</span>. Nineteen icons cover the product; an icon always sits beside a word or carries an aria-label. No emoji anywhere; ▲ and ▼ are text glyphs for change, not icons.

7Voice
