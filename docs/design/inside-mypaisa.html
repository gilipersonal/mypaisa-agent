How MyPaisa works, in plain words

From the first tap to a team of agents keeping the family's money.

MyPaisa is a private money assistant for one household. You talk to it in a chat, on the iPhone, the iPad or in a browser. It keeps everything the family owns and owes in the UK and India, works out how it has grown (XIRR and CAGR from the dated ledger), forecasts where it is heading from those actual numbers, asks when something is due, and explains what moved. This page follows the household's admin from the very first screen to the chatbot at work, and shows what happens behind every tap.

Every figure comes from tested code. Claude, the AI, only chooses what to say and how to say it.

2

No broker log-in and no orders. It records and explains; it never tells you to buy or sell, and it can never move money.

Every message has a simple top. The technical side opens only when you ask for it.

4

Admins write, in the chat

Only admins add, correct and delete. Adding and deleting happen only in the chat, with a Confirm tap; an admin may also correct a figure where it shows. Everyone else views.

Open the app and sign in

The first screen shows two buttons and nothing else: Sign in with Apple and Sign in with Google. Arun taps Apple, looks at the phone, and he is in. There is no password to make, forget or leak.

  • Behind the screen
  • A one-time code first. Before the Apple sheet opens, the app fetches a code that can be used once, so a stolen sign-in can't be replayed. /auth/nonce

  • The ID desk checks the signature against Apple's published keys, then issues a pass that lasts five minutes and renews itself quietly. Cognito custom auth
  • The pass stays on this device only, in the iPhone's Keychain. On the web it is a cookie that page scripts can never read.

Phase 2AI understandsCode checks

Add something by just saying it

Arun types "£5,000 in Monzo savings". MyPaisa asks only what it can't work out (whose is it?), shows a card with exactly what it will save, and waits for Confirm. The same works for a fixed deposit in India, a mortgage, a pension or a car loan: one sentence, a question or two, one tap. Only an admin can add, and only in the chat.

  • Behind the screen
  • Claude reads the sentence and passes each part, in Arun's own words, to the drafting tool. draft_record
  • Code turns words into checked values, works out anything it can (an EMI, a maturity date), and lists what is missing. A draft writes nothing.
  • Only the missing bits are asked, one at a time with chips. It never asks what it could look up.
  • Confirm saves exactly the card you saw, and the change and its audit line are written together or not at all.
  • Deleting is in the chat too, admins only: a card, Confirm and a second check. The record is marked deleted, hidden from every view and restorable through the chat; its history, audit lines and files are kept for good.

Phases 2 to 9Code computes

See the whole picture, and fix a figure where it shows

Pull the chat down and Home opens: the household's net worth in pounds and rupees, what it owns and owes, each person's share, UK against India, then Assets, Debts, Budget and the Projection. On the laptop, the same dashboard sits beside the chat. Arun, as an admin, taps his home's value, types the new estimate, sees before and after, and taps Save. The laptop moves at the same moment. A member who is not an admin sees the same figures, read only.

  • Behind the screen
  • Figures are views, rebuilt from history. Every record keeps a list of what happened to it; net worth is worked out from those, never typed in.
  • A correction is a new line, not an overwrite. It is saved as a correction with its audit line, and the old value stays in the history. via: inline_edit
  • Only figures their kind marks editable can be tapped. Derived figures (an EMI, net worth) are not editable; their inputs are.
  • Two screens never overwrite each other. Save carries the figure it showed and is refused if it has moved since. A saved change reaches every open screen on the live line.

Phase 4Decided by code, no AI

MyPaisa asks you, so you don't have to remember

Every loan EMI, card bill, SIP, premium, salary and maturity is a promise with a date. On the day, a check-in arrives: "HDFC home loan: was November's EMI paid?" The card already shows exactly what a tap will save, so Paid is the Confirm, with Undo. "Not yet" asks again in a few days; "Paid off in full" closes the loan and reruns the Projection.

  • Behind the screen
  • A daily job, plain code, finds what is due and puts each check-in straight in the out-tray. No AI is needed to know a date has come.
  • Asked once, never twice. Each check-in has an id made from the promise and its date, so a retry can't ask again. commitment#due_date
  • No amounts on the lock screen, unless you turn that on.
  • Autopay is assumed paid after its grace days and listed once a week, which keeps the household to about ten notifications a week.

Send a statement; the budget fills itself

Phase 7AI explainsCode computes

Ask anything: the chatbot at work

"What if we put £60,000 down on a second flat in June 2028?" MyPaisa answers in plain words with a chart: the household's net worth year by year with that deposit, its likely range, and the plan as it stands for comparison. It's a what-if, so nothing is saved unless Arun, as admin, chooses to add it to the plan. "Why?" and "Technical" open the reasoning and the assumptions, each with its source.

  • Behind the screen, in about two seconds
  • The front desk says "got it" at once and drops the question in Arun's own lane of the in-tray.
  • The concierge asks Claude what's needed. Claude picks two tools: read the figures, and run the projection with this change.
  • The calculator runs 20 years forward from the household's actual numbers (XIRR and CAGR from the dated ledger, salaries, debts and plans), with a low, middle and high path. Code makes every number.
  • Claude writes the answer around those numbers, and the words appear on screen as they are written.

Safety nets

What stops it going wrong

A money app for a family has to be boring in the right places. These are the guard rails, written into the code rather than into the AI's instructions, so the AI can't talk its way around them.

Guarded in code, tested on every change

Before any tool runs, a small piece of code checks it is allowed for this agent and this person, that its details make sense and that the run is inside its limits; if in doubt, the answer is no. What a statement, a factsheet or a web page says is read as data, never as an instruction, and a chat that has read one loses every tool that could send something out. Every figure in a reply must come from a tool or from your own words.

The tests run on every change, never on a timer: recorded ones always, live ones when the AI's instructions or tools change, and a new Claude model is tested five times over before it answers you. Every live chat is checked by code, and a few are read overnight by a judge that you calibrate each month. The whole story, in plain words or technical detail: MyPaisa Guardrails and Evals.

A spending ceiling for the AI, with a ladder

The admin sets a monthly ceiling for Claude, $160 in prod by default, paid from the plan's monthly credit. Two members are expected to use about $24 of it. Every call's cost is added to a meter, and the meter is read before every call.

70%A message to the admin. Nothing changes.

100%The AI stops until the month renews or the ceiling is raised. Buttons, check-ins and alerts still work, since they need no AI.

Nothing is lost, everything is retried

Every hand-over goes through a tray (a queue). If Apple's push service, Claude or a price source has a bad minute, the work waits and tries again, a little later each time. After too many tries it goes into safe keeping for 14 days, with a copy kept for good, and the admin gets an alert with a Replay button. Every part remembers what it has done, so a retry never does anything twice.

Things no part of MyPaisa can do

  • Trade or move money. There is no broker connection at all.
  • Erase data. A loan is closed, a fund is sold; a delete, admin only and in the chat, marks a record deleted and keeps its history.
  • Message anyone but the member it is answering.
  • Save a draft. Only a Confirm, a check-in tap or a Save commits.
  • See another household. Every item is filed by household, and every tool call carries who is asking.
  • Keep running when told to stop. Pause MyPaisa in You, or type /pause, and everything scheduled stops.
