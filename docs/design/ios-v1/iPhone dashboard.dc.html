The read-only screens of MyPaisa's Home tab, in option 1a's shell (MA-161, MA-78): Chat · Home · Activity · You and the search circle, Home selected. Warm ledger is the content: the navy hero, the cards, the grouped lists and the charts are solid; Liquid Glass is only the toolbar buttons, the tab bar and the partial sheets. Every figure is the engine's, nothing here can trade, and every change still starts in the chat: a screen's Ask and Update open it with the flow already started. The same read-only screens, with the same figures, are also on the web app (a PWA at console(-dev).mypaisapp.com). Each board is shown in light and dark; long screens are drawn as they open and scrolled to the end; each frame plays its motion as it scrolls into view (replay beside its label). Planned: each goes through design-first when its story starts. Illustrative figures and names.
