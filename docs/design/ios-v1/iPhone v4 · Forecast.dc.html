The stock Forecaster (ARCHITECTURE §19) on a holding’s screen, drawn for the app for the first time, in shell 1a with the Liquid Glass tab bar, and on iPad with the sidebar and the chat beside it. Asha’s NASDAQ 100 ETF, at the Fri 23 Oct close, the Forecaster in practice, week 5 of 8. Three answers to one screen: 1a the answer first, then the chart and its patterns, then why; 1b the chart is the screen, with a layer for each model; 1c the questions a member asks, each answered in one line and opened in place.
