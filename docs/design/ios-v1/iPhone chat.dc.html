CThe chat in the app: seventeen boards in 1a's shell

The conversation on iPhone (MA-169), in option 1a's shell: the household strip at the top, the composer and its shortcut chips, the tab bar with Chat selected. MyPaisa's words sit beside its mark, the member's messages are navy, and every card is drawn natively as solid Warm ledger with its own buttons; Liquid Glass is only the layer of controls. The content is carried over from the Telegram-era boards and ARCHITECTURE §9.2, and each board is drawn in light and dark. Illustrative figures and names.
