iOS v1The mark in motion, on iPhone and iPad

How MyPaisa’s mark moves in the app. Arun picked 1a, Watcher at work, on 25 Nov 2026 (MA-355, item d), and said the same day that on iPhone the mark beside the reply being written is the one that moves. So: the arrival plays at launch, on the full drawing; the lap and the ring play in the chat, on the mark beside MyPaisa’s newest reply. The coin stays put; only the dot travels.

The motion itself, its tokens and the web sidebar are on Mark in motion in MyPaisa DS v1; this board draws where it happens on iPhone and iPad. Every stage is the real animation; Play again runs it, and the strips are the same animation paused at the times shown.

What it supersedes. It extends Logo’s “App opening” frames for 1c (MA-103’s intro, drawn in a Telegram Mini App that no longer exists) with the app’s own launch, and it adds motion to the mark beside a reply, which the chat boards draw still.

When it stays still

Privacy mode has no effect. The mark holds no figure, so it looks and moves the same with amounts hidden; the app switcher still shows only the still mark.

The mark is not a control. It is decorative beside a message: no tap, no long press, no focus, and VoiceOver passes over it. The reply’s own words are what is read.

Only the newest reply’s mark moves. The marks beside older messages, and the mark in the household strip, stay still while it does.

Nothing else starts it. Not a timer, not a tab change, not a notification, not a figure rolling. A reply that streams for a long time gets one lap, at its start.

The mark beside a message is the mark itself. These frames draw the small cut, 28 pt, as the web app does, where the iOS kit draws a ringed dot (.ai-mark): a ringed dot has no seat to leave and no coin to go round. The other chat boards still show the ringed dot.
