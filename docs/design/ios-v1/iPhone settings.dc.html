What arrives, and how. The 07:15 brief and the 21:15 wrap are ordinary notifications; a red alert is time-sensitive with sound, at most two a week (MA-59); amber is silent; the weekly digest comes on Saturdays.

Quiet hours, 22:00 to 07:00, hold for everything but a critical alert (MA-170).

Rule 4 holds here. These are preferences, so a switch or a time does not save itself: it opens the chat with the preference card ready (“New preference · check it”), and nothing changes until Confirm, with an audit row (MA-69). The banner on top says so.

Ways in, one person. Apple (used on this iPhone), Google and a passkey with Face ID can all sit on the same Cognito user (MA-109); there is still no password. The same sign-in opens the read-only web app at console.mypaisapp.com.

Add is a fresh sign-in with that account, in Apple's or Google's own sheet or iOS's passkey sheet; the api links its id to you, with an audit row (MA-77). An added passkey can also answer the second check (MA-76).

The household's kill switch. Pause MyPaisa is /pause: every scheduled run stops, so no brief, wrap or alert (red ones too) and no price or engine runs. Records, the chat and Home stay, with the last prices.

It acts at once, with no Confirm, because a kill switch must never wait; it is audited and every member is told. Resume is /resume.

Who can: only Arun, the owner, pauses or resumes the household; a member sees the paused state and who paused it. The Telegram board let anyone resume; MA-35's options round settles that.

Not the other pauses: the PAUSE stance is a view on one holding, and pausing a member is the Admin center's (MA-102). Keys MA-35.

What leaving does, said first: the sign-in is deleted, Apple's refresh token revoked through Apple's endpoint (Google's grant too), the accounts and history stay with the household, the audit rows keep the member's id and the day they left, and Arun is told (MA-172).

Red, and behind the second check. Leave is destructive, so it is alert red. It opens iOS's Sign in with Apple sheet under Face ID, bound to this action and checked by the server (MA-76); a Google-only member signs in with Google again. Cancel and the sheet's close change nothing.

The record. Arun's chat gets the card with its audit row; Asha's iPhone goes back to the welcome. A what-if: Asha leaving shows the flow; the other boards keep her.

The owner cannot leave while they are the only owner: the screen says so and Leave is off. Keys MA-172, MA-76.
