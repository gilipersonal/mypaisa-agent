AiPhone audit and activity: every change, every file, every run

The two screens of record in the app (MA-161, MA-71, MA-79), in option 1a's shell with the Activity tab selected: Activity says what MyPaisa did today and what it cost; Audit, opened from Activity's toolbar or from the chat, lists every change with who made it, when, through what and the run behind it, and opens the file behind a change in Quick Look through a five-minute link (MA-149). Both are read-only: the filters only narrow, Open only reads, and every change still starts as a card and a Confirm tap in the chat. The same screens, with the same rows and runs, are also on the web app (a PWA at console(-dev).mypaisapp.com). Each frame plays its motion as it scrolls into view (replay beside its label). Warm ledger content under Liquid Glass bars, light and dark. Planned: each goes through design-first when its story starts. Illustrative figures and names.
