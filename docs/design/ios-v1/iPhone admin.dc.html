What is stuck, and what it holds up. The queue, the job, its tries, the reason, and that the source answers again, which is when to replay. Silver itself is priced: the 07:00 run fell back to yfinance.

Replay is coral: it moves the job back and stops nothing. Handlers are idempotent, and it asks for the second check like every action here (ARCHITECTURE §21.3).

The result, not just the act. Replayed says the job ran and what it produced, with the audit row; the Admin center's badge clears and a message in the chat says the same.

Replayed and failed again is back in the dead-letter queue and the alarm fires again. Nothing replays on its own, so a replay can never loop; Replay turns plain until the source answers (MA-145).
