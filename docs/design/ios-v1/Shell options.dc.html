1The app's shell: three ways the chat and the dashboard meet

MyPaisa as one iOS app (MA-161, MA-163). Every option has the same screens (the chat and its cards, Home, a holding, Audit, Activity, the Admin center, You); they differ in what the app opens to and how the chat and the figures meet. Warm ledger (MA-101) is the content; Liquid Glass is only the layer of controls above it: the tab bar, the sidebar, toolbar buttons, the composer and its shortcut chips. iPhone in light and dark, then iPad in landscape. The flow boards in this project are drawn in 1a's shell, which Arun picked on 26 Oct 2026; 1b and 1c stay as the record. Illustrative figures and names.

Opens to the chat. The conversation is the front door. The household's total and the day's move ride in a glass strip at the top; pull it down, or tap it, and Home opens as a sheet over the chat.

Tabs: Chat · Home · Activity · You, with Search as iOS 26's separate search tab. As the conversation scrolls the tab bar shrinks to its selected tab, so the composer has the bottom to itself.

The composer is glass; the shortcut chips above it change with the conversation (Portfolio, Today, Add a holding, Log money). The + opens the camera, a document scan, Photos and Files.

Cards are content, solid Warm ledger with their own buttons. Confirm is a filled content button, never glass.

Picked by Arun, 26 Oct 2026: Arun asked for "a cool mobile chatbot ... and even see the dashboard". Every change starts where it is made, in the chat, and the figures are one swipe away.

Motion. Messages arrive in turn, the chips stagger in and the total rolls up. On iPad the chat column slides in from the trailing edge when the window widens past 900 pt, and a rotation morphs the layout with matched geometry, no reload.

Opens to Home. The dashboard is the app; the chat is the tab bar's bottom accessory, the way Music carries its mini player, on every tab.

It carries the screen's context: on Home it reads "Ask MyPaisa about Home", on a holding "Ask about Balanced". A tap expands it into the full chat with that context already set.

Tabs: Home · Holdings · Activity · You, with Search apart. The Admin center sits under You for the owner.

Best when the household mostly reads. Adding money or a factsheet is a tap further away than in 1a: open the accessory, then type.

Motion. The accessory grows into the full chat with a glass morph, and shrinks back on the way out. On iPad a rotation or a resize morphs the layout with matched geometry, no reload.

No tabs: the chat is the app. A glass rail of live cards rides above the conversation: Portfolio, Today, Alerts (when there are any), Spend for the owner. Each opens its screen as a sheet.

Everything else is a sheet or a chip: Audit, Activity, the Admin center and You open from the chips or the account button.

The purest chatbot, and the most cramped for the owner: the Admin center's settings and the Audit filters live in sheets over the conversation.

Motion. The pinned cards rise in turn and the messages arrive one by one; a pinned card opens its sheet from where it sits. On iPad a rotation morphs the layout with matched geometry, no reload.
