MyPaisa at regular width, in option 1a's shell (MA-163). The layout follows the window's width alone, never the device: under about 600 pt it is the iPhone's (the tab bar at the bottom, one column); from about 600 to 900 pt the sidebar collapses into a tab bar at the top and the chat is a sheet; over about 900 pt the glass sidebar, the screen and the chat sit side by side. "Duo" is read as a foldable or two-panel Apple device, folded or open. Each board in light and dark. Illustrative figures and names.
