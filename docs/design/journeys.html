How a layman uses the bot, scene by scene

Eight journeys through the Telegram bot, played on a phone. MyPaisa is a wealth tracker driven by the chat: it keeps the household's records, its budget and its wealth forecast from the household's own numbers. Each journey shows what you tap, what comes back, and what happens behind it. Nothing technical appears unless you ask; when you do, it is one tap away. These are mock-ups of the design; nothing is built yet.

MA-307, 2 Nov 2026: the market side was retired; MyPaisa is a wealth tracker only.

    A week in the life

    About five messages a week from the bot, at most two with sound. Quiet 22:00–07:00. Everything else waits until you ask.

    MONnothing unless something is dueYou log "£42 dinner out" in words
    TUE🟡 groceries at 90% of plan, silent

    THUYou ask "how are my investments doing?" at lunch
    FRI🔴 a failed price job, to the admin only
    SATYou open the dashboard
    SUNAn admin sends a screenshot after topping up

    The first months

    Day 1
    An admin opens the chat, picks GBP, sends four factsheets and one screenshot. Five holdings in the registry after five Confirm taps. The Portfolio card shows your own figures.
    Day 2
    The first daily prices value the holdings. The dashboard shows net worth, each holding's value and the month's budget.
    Week 2
    You ask your first question about your wealth. The technical section is there if you want it; most days you don't.
    Month 1
    The first month closes on the 1st: the budget against its plan, and the month's line of the Budget forecast graded against what happened.
    Month 6
    Six closed months: the estimates behind the Projection (each category's run rate, its inflation, salary growth) can be refitted from the household's own history, a step at a time, with an admin deciding.
    Any day
    An admin sends a factsheet for a new ETF, a share or a commodity; it is valued from the next close. Say "only tell me when a budget line is over" and confirm. /pause stops everything; /privacy on hides pounds.

    Rules every journey keeps

    Plain on top, detail on tapEvery card reads in two lines. "Technical ▾" opens the numbers; a glossary sits behind each term.
    Only admins writeThe household can have several admins. Admins add and delete only in the chat, with a card and Confirm; a delete takes Confirm and the second check (a fresh sign-in with Apple, Google or a passkey), marks the record deleted (restorable through the chat) and writes an audit row. Admins correct a figure in the chat or in place on a screen. Every other member views only.
    Numbers from code, words from ClaudeEvery figure in a message came out of a tool; Claude only chooses what to say.
    Forecasts from your own numbersThe Projection and the Budget forecast come from the household's actual numbers: XIRR and CAGR from the dated ledger, salaries, debts and plans. Nothing predicts a market; nothing says buy or sell.
    It cannot tradeNo broker login exists. Daily prices only value what you hold.
    Silence is a featureAlerts only for a budget line, a check-in due or a failed job; a weekly budget of two loud ones, quiet hours, and a filter that drops repeats.
