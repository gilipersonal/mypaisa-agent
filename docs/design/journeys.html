    The first months

    Day 1
    Open the chat, pick GBP, send four factsheets and one screenshot. Five holdings in the registry after five Confirm taps. The Portfolio card shows your own figures.
    Day 2
    The first morning brief, from yesterday's closes and the fund's NAV. Stances are computed but shown nowhere yet.
    Week 2
    You ask your first "why?". The technical section is there if you want it; most days you don't.
    Week 4
    The first Saturday review with a scorecard: did the 80% ranges cover 80% of outcomes?
    Week 8
    Stances have been paper-traded for eight weeks. If they beat doing nothing, ADD / HOLD / PAUSE chips appear on weekday cards. If not, the review says so and they stay in the Saturday review.
    Any day
    Send a factsheet for a new ETF, a share or a commodity; it is priced from the next close. Say "stop pinging me under 2%" and confirm. /pause stops everything; /privacy on hides pounds.

    Rules every journey keeps

    Plain on top, detail on tapEvery card reads in two lines. "Technical ▾" opens the numbers; a glossary sits behind each term.
    Nothing saved without ConfirmA misread factsheet or a mistyped amount cannot quietly change your records.
    Numbers from code, words from ClaudeEvery figure in a message came out of a tool; Claude only chooses what to say.
    A view, not advice; never "sell everything"Stances are ADD, HOLD, PAUSE, TRIM or REBALANCE, with a confidence that is checked every Saturday.
    It cannot tradeNo broker login exists. You act in the Monzo app and tell the bot "I acted".
    Silence is a featureCooldowns, a weekly budget of two loud alerts, quiet hours, and a filter that drops repeats.
