MA-117The Forecaster: phase 11, beside the rules engine

The Forecaster is a second engine that learns from ten years of history and sits beside the rules engine without replacing it (ARCHITECTURE.md §19). These boards are where a member meets it: a Forecast tab on each holding in the browser and the Mini App, the side-by-side card in Telegram, a second suggested split on the first-of-the-month prompt, and the page that shows how it proves itself. Built on the Browser, Mini App and Telegram chats boards in MyPaisa DS v1, Warm ledger; every board in light and dark. Illustrative figures from ARCHITECTURE §19.7 and docs/design/market-agent.html, a later week than the Browser boards, so the rules engine reads PAUSE 64% on the NASDAQ 100 ETF. "You" is the owner and Asha a fictional member.
