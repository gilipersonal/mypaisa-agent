Motion in this prototype First open of a tab: cards rise 10 px and fade in, 40 ms apart 350ms ease(0.22,1,0.36,1). Back and tab taps never replay it. The total counts up from zero once, on first open 600ms; the chart fades in 200ms and its stance dots after it. A chart line is never dash-drawn: that breaks under non-scaling strokes. Push: both screens stay on screen --mp-t-push 350ms, --mp-ease-sheet; the new one slides in from the right while the old one slides 30% left and dims. Back reverses it. Tabs switch at once, and each tab keeps its place: its pushed page and its scroll position. Why? sheet: slides up over a dimmed screen 420ms, below Telegram's bar, which is not ours to dim; ✕, the scrim or Esc close it 280ms. Rules: transform and opacity only; nothing loops; with Reduce Motion on, every screen lands in its final state at once and the total shows without counting.
