MA-102Telegram second check: five journeys

Today a Telegram session is trusted on the Telegram account alone. These screens add a second check on the phone: Face ID through Telegram’s BiometricManager, or a 6-digit MyPaisa PIN where Face ID isn’t available (a phone with a fingerprint reader says fingerprint). The check runs when MyPaisa opens after 12 hours away and after every Confirm on a change; reading stays one tap. Every phone is shown in Telegram light and Telegram dark; in the chat, a tapped button is ringed. Design system: MyPaisa DS v1, Warm ledger. Illustrative figures; "You" is the owner and Asha a fictional member.
