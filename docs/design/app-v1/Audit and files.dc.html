FAudit and files: every file kept, and opened from the log

Arun, 24 Oct 2026: “all the files and images I send should be stored on S3 and I should be able to view that in audit logs.” Every file a member sends is kept for good and opens from the log through a link that works for five minutes, after a role check: a member opens their own files, the owner any, a viewer none, and every opening is itself an audit row (ARCHITECTURE.md §4.6, §14). These boards extend what exists rather than redraw it: Browser 2's Audit (B6) and the Mini App's Audit keep their shell, grid and filters, and gain a Changes | Files switch and a file line under any row made from a file. Drawn at 1280 (wide: sidebar, no tab bar) and at 390 inside Telegram, light and dark from one markup. Read-only: Open only reads, and nothing here deletes, renames or uploads. Illustrative data: the members are You (the owner) and Asha (a member), and every file name, size and figure is made up.
