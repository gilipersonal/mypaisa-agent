1Responsive · one Overview, five widths

The web app is adaptive: one markup, three of Apple's size classes, decided by the width of the window it is in (a phone, Telegram Desktop's Mini App window, an iPad, a laptop), not by the device. Every frame below is the same markup in a container of that width; nothing is drawn twice by hand. The rules live at the end of tokens.css (.mp-app, .mp-shell, .mp-cols). In the Mini App, Telegram's own header replaces the large title; everything under it is this markup. Illustrative figures.

Compact · under 600Phone browser and the Mini App. One column, in summary order: hero, this week, holdings, stats. Bottom tab bar. Large title on its own row, controls under it. Gutters 16.
Regular · 600 to 1023Telegram Desktop, iPad split view, iPad portrait. Tab bar stays (touch). Title and controls share a row. One column until each of two can be about 340 wide (760); then two equal columns, the story left and the list right. Gutters 24.
Wide · 1024 and upLaptop. A 248 sidebar replaces the tab bar; columns 1.3 : 1, the list never under 300; stats stay 2 × 2; content capped at 1180 and centred past 1360. Gutters 32.
