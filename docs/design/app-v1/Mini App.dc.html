1Mini App · every screen · light and dark

Direction 1a, "Warm ledger" (MyPaisa DS v1). The Mini App opens from the Portfolio card in the chat and follows Telegram's theme. Telegram draws the top bar: Close on a tab's root, and on a pushed page a Back that names where it goes (‹ Holdings, ‹ More). MyPaisa draws everything below it. Five tabs, as Apple's tab bar: Overview, Holdings, Overlap, Record and More, a list root for Activity, Audit and About. A pushed page keeps the tab it came from selected, and the tab bar stays on it. Long screens are drawn twice, as they open and scrolled to the end; every screen keeps 104 px of bottom padding, so nothing ends behind the tab bar. Read-only: every change happens in the chat. Illustrative figures; "Asha" is a fictional member. The same Overview at tablet and laptop widths is on the Responsive page.
