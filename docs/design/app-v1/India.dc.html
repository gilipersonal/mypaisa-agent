INIndia: Indian funds, NSE shares and ETFs, and the import of an Indian portfolio

Phase 13, epic MA-147 (ARCHITECTURE.md §22): Indian mutual funds and NSE and BSE shares and ETFs sit beside the UK holdings, and the household's existing Indian portfolio is imported once from its statements. Six boards, each in light and dark, drawn in MyPaisa DS v1 "Warm ledger" on the patterns of the Browser chats boards. Every Indian figure is in rupees with its pound value beside it, at the ECB's reference rate for the day (here £1 = ₹112.50, Monday 2 November, the one illustrative rate every MyPaisa board uses); the household total stays in pounds, with the rupee view one tap away; privacy mode hides rupees as it hides pounds. The web app stays read-only: every change is a card and a Confirm in the chat, and the one field on these boards, a statement's password, is used once in memory and never stored. Illustrative figures throughout: "You" (the owner) and "Asha" (a member) are fictional, the Indian funds are described rather than named, and every folio, account number and ID is masked.
