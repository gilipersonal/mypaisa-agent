The one exception to read-onlyEverywhere else the web app only reads, and every change is a card and a Confirm tap in the chat. The Admin center's owner actions are the one exception: sign out a session or everywhere, pause or restore a Telegram account (the owner's own too), unlink Google. Nothing here can touch a holding or the history.
What a fresh check isProof that the owner is at this keyboard now: their passkey (Touch ID on this Mac) or a fresh Google sign-in. It lasts 5 minutes; after that, the next action asks again. Reading the page needs none. Every action is also a row in the security log and a Telegram message to the member and the owner.
What MyPaisa can and cannot endIt can end its own sessions: a browser, a Mini App. It cannot sign anyone out of Telegram itself; it can only stop trusting the account. Paused, the bot answers one line, the Mini App locks and every MyPaisa session ends, browsers too, until the owner restores it. The owner can pause their own account and gets back in with Google or a passkey. A lost phone's Telegram is ended in Telegram's own Settings, Devices.
