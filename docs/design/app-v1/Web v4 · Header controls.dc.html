Arun picked this on Wed 25 Nov 2026 (MA-355, item b, option 1a, four labelled pills at the top right; docs/design/reviews/MA-355), from his reference of three labelled pills. This board is the spec: the desktop at 1440 and 1280, the tightest widths, and the phone’s browser at 390, in light and dark, with each menu open and each state a build needs.

It supersedes the head of Web v4 · Dashboard (the three-part Household, Me, Asha switch and the eye button) and the £ GBP, ₹ INR switch inside its navy hero, on every frame of that board; and the eye button in every other Web v4 page’s head. Home’s cards below the head are today’s, unchanged here (the new chart cards are MA-355 item c’s board). The chat beside the page is gone from these frames: the desktop chat is a sheet (MA-355 item a).

Keys: Tab reaches each pill in order; Enter, Space or the down arrow opens a menu with focus on the row in force; the arrows move, Enter chooses and closes, Escape closes and returns focus to the pill.

Roles: each menu is a menu of radio items (shadcn-svelte DropdownMenu with a radio group); Hide amounts is a button with aria-pressed.

System is the default: the pill then reads System and shows the sun or the moon of the moment, and the page follows the device.

Dollars and euros are named, and no frame shows a figure in them: the figure set holds only £1 = ₹112.50.

Motion: a menu scales in from its pill (the shared menu role); under Reduce Motion it appears in place.

3Hide amounts, rupees, and Me

What each control does to the page. Hide amounts masks every figure at its own width and the pill turns to ink and reads Show amounts. ₹ INR turns every total to rupees, in lakh and crore, while each record keeps its own currency. Me shows the member’s own records and their share of the joint ones.
