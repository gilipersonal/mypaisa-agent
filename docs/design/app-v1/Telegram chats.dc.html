TTelegram chats: three journeys

The bot's messages as a member sees them, so the words and their order can be judged on a real chat. Telegram draws the chat; MyPaisa owns the words, the buttons that appear and the PNG images (navy, coral line). A tapped inline button is ringed; tapping one sends nothing to the chat, it either edits that message or the bot answers with a new one. One story in time order: Friday's wrap (a0), Friday's red alert (c), Monday's brief (a1 to a3); journey b is the Monday before. Each in Telegram light and dark. Illustrative figures; the components are in the DS project's Telegram kit.
