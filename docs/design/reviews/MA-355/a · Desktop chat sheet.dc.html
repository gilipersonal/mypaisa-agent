Why recommended: it is what you asked for, drawn as the iPad draws it (600 pt, 56 from the top, 20 from the bottom, the page dimmed), so the web and the iPad show one chat. The page keeps its full width at every size, and one rule replaces two bands (the drawer, the column).

1024 to 1392 px: the sheet, in place of today’s drawer. At 1280 the page is 1,032 px with or without the chat.

From 1393 px: the same sheet; no chat column. At 1440 the page is its full 1,180 px, where today the column leaves it about 788.

Opens: Chat in the sidebar, ⌘J, an Update or Ask on a page, a check-in’s notification, or a question typed into ⌘K. The caret lands in the composer.

Closes: Esc, the close button, a click on the dimmed page, or the grabber pulled down. The conversation and a half-typed message are kept.

Trade-off: the page cannot be read or corrected while the chat is open; after a Confirm you close the sheet to see the page change, as on iPad. On a very wide window the sheet stays 600 px, so more of the dimmed page shows around it.

Build: AppShell.svelte loses the wide/drawer split and the hidden-column setting; shell.css swaps the 1393 and 1024 to 1392 blocks for one sheet and a scrim; ChatPanel gets one mode, a dialog with a focus trap.

What changed since Sunday?⌘J

Strength: the chat is always one keystroke away and never hidden behind a menu item, which suits a product where everything new is made in the chat. The sheet is the widest of the three, so the plan card is shortest.

1024 to 1392 px: the bar and its sheet, centred on the page (not the window); at 1280 the sheet is 720 of the page’s 1,032 px.

From 1393 px: the same; no chat column, so the 1393 px rule goes on the web, as in 1a.

Opens: typing in the bar, the bar’s chevron, ⌘J, or Chat in the sidebar. Return sends and raises the sheet.

Closes: Esc, the chevron in the sheet’s header, the grabber pulled down, or a click on the page. It lowers back to the bar; nothing is dimmed, so the sidebar and the page’s header work while it is up.

Trade-off: the bar covers the foot of every page for good (each page needs about 100 px of clearance), and it is the ask bar you passed over when you picked 1a Chat beside in MA-193. It is less like your picture: the sheet is centred on the page and nothing dims.
