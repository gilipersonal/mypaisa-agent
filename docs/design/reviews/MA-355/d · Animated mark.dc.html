MA-355MA-355 · d · The mark, animated

Arun, 25 Nov 2026: “Make the logo animated on top left corner. It should be engaging.” The mark is 1c Watcher (MA-103): a navy coin, a white pound, and a coral dot sitting in a bite at its top right, the agent keeping watch. In the web app it sits at the top left of the sidebar, 32 px beside the name, and today it does not move.

The motion rules decide what “engaging” can mean here. Nothing loops forever, so a mark that keeps moving in the corner of a finance app is out. Each option gives the mark a reason to move, plays once and ends as the same still mark. In all three the coin stays put and only the dot travels: the coin is the household’s money, the dot is the agent.

Every stage is the real animation, in CSS, on the design system’s own springs; Play again runs it, and the strips under it are the same animation paused at the times shown. The mark is the sidebar’s small cut, at its true 32 px and enlarged to 160 px so the movement can be judged.

Why recommended. It is the only one of the three where the movement tells you something. The mark is the Watcher: it moves when MyPaisa starts work for you and when your change is written down, a few times in a session, and it is still the rest of the time. That is “engaging” that a finance app can live with every day.

When it can fire. On open, once. One lap per message you send. One ring per save (a Confirm in the chat, or a Save in place on a page). One reaction at a time: a save that lands during a lap plays when the lap ends; anything else that arrives while one is playing is dropped, never queued. Nothing fires on its own, on a timer, or while the tab is hidden.

Trade-off. At 1440 the chat is on the right and the mark is top left, so the lap is seen from the corner of the eye. It is an acknowledgement, not the progress signal: the chat’s own “Reading it…” stays the place to look. In the chat, the mark beside each message stays still on the web, so only one mark moves.

iOS. The same three moments. Launch: the arrival, on the full drawing with its orbit, as MA-103 picked. In the chat: the mark beside the reply being written makes the lap when MyPaisa starts, and rings on Saved with the success haptic. SwiftUI .smooth, .snappy and .bouncy; under Reduce Motion the same cross-fades as here. On iPhone there is no sidebar, so the chat’s mark is the one that moves.

Build. Mark.svelte gains its moving parts and a play(open | lap | saved); AppShell.svelte calls it from the chat session’s events (a turn starting, a record saved) and from an in-place Save; the keyframes go in shell.css beside .mpm. The bite becomes a mask that travels with the dot, so it works on any ground.

What it is. MA-103’s pick, finished: “a new MyPaisa mark that animates once when the app opens”. The web sidebar never got that intro, so this builds it and stops there.

Strength. The least to go wrong. One second, once, while the page is arriving anyway; after that the corner never moves, which is what a ledger should do. Nothing to wire to the chat.

Trade-off. It is engaging for one second per visit. In a tab left open all day it is seen once in the morning, and a returning member may not be looking at the corner when it plays. It answers “animated”; it only half answers “engaging”.

iOS. The launch screen plays the same arrival on the full drawing with its orbit (MA-103). The chat’s mark never moves.

Build. Mark.svelte gains its moving parts and plays once on mount in the shell; keyframes in shell.css. The smallest of the three.

What it is. The arrival on open, then a mark that is still until you go to it. It becomes a link to Home (it is not a link today), so the movement is the hover of a real control, not a toy.

Strength. It can never distract: it only moves when the pointer is already there. A small reward for the curious, and the link Home is useful on its own.

Trade-off. Most people never hover a logo, so most will never see it; on a phone or a tablet without a pointer it is 1b. It also means nothing: the dot goes round because you pointed, not because MyPaisa did anything.

iOS. iPhone has no pointer, so this is 1b there: the launch arrival and nothing else. On iPad with a trackpad the sidebar’s mark could answer the pointer the same way.

Build. AppShell.svelte wraps the brand in a link to Home with a 44 px target and the focus ring; Mark.svelte gains its moving parts; the lap plays from pointerenter and focus; keyframes in shell.css.
