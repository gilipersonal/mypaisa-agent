The mark, animated in the top left corner

Your words: “Make the logo animated on top left corner. It should be engaging.” The motion rules say nothing loops forever, so each option moves for a reason and then is still. In all three the coin stays put and only the coral dot travels, with its bite.

1a · Watcher at work. It arrives once when the app opens (the coin settles, the pound writes itself, the dot comes round the rim into its bite), then moves only when MyPaisa does something for you: one lap of the dot when it starts on your message, and one swell with a coral ring when a change is saved. Every movement tells you something, a few times a session.

Trade-off: At 1440 the lap happens at the top left while your eyes are in the chat, and it needs wiring to the chat’s events.

1b · Arrives once. Only the arrival, once per load: MA-103’s pick, “animates once when the app opens”, built for the sidebar. The smallest build and the least to go wrong.

Trade-off: Engaging for about one second a visit, and easily missed.

1c · Answers the pointer. The arrival, then one lap when the pointer or keyboard focus reaches the mark, which becomes a link to Home. It can never distract.

Trade-off: Most people never hover a logo, on touch it is 1b, and the movement means nothing.

If picked, it changes: Mark.svelte (split into moving parts), shell.css and AppShell.svelte on the web; the launch screen and, for 1a, the mark beside a reply on iOS; the boards ds-v1 · Logo and Web v4 · Shell and chat. Yours to settle with the pick: in 1a, should the mark beside a reply in the web chat move too (drawn still, so one mark moves at a time); in 1c, should the logo become a link to Home at all.
