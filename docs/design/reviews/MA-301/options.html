The deep scan’s redesign items, as options

Arun asked to complete the tasks from antigravity for the UI WORK and to push them to claude artifact. Seven items from the deep scan need a choice before anything is built. Each has two or three options, the recommended one first; pick one per item.

Boards are drawn in MyPaisa DS v1 (Warm ledger) in light and dark, at the sizes each item covers, with every figure from the one illustrative household. They scroll sideways inside their frame at desktop sizes.

a

Projection and Plans: ten controls above the answer

P2-3. Three tabs, four horizons, two currencies and a switch sit in two rows above the Projection and the Plans tab on iPhone, and as tabs plus segments on the web.

1a · One picker, one lens. One segmented picker for the three views and one 44 pt lens, “20 yrs · £ GBP · Nominal”, that opens a single menu. The answer moves up a row; the lens still names the view.

Trade-off: Changing the horizon takes two taps instead of one.

1b · The title is the picker. The view becomes the title’s menu, horizon chips sit under the fan, money is a corner button on the hero.

Trade-off: A title menu is easy to miss; Plans and Budget forecast hide behind it; chips add weight to the navy hero.

1c · One row of chips. All six choices as chips in one row, three views then three menus.

Trade-off: The row scrolls on iPhone and cuts Nominal; views and settings look alike.

b

The statement review: one card four screens tall

P2-3. Board I2 on iPhone v4 · Budget draws October’s statements as one Confirm card with sources, two amber merchants, eleven category tiles and five recognised lines, Confirm at the very bottom.

1a · Questions first, then a short Confirm. Each uncertain merchant gets its own question card (the guided-flow pattern), then a compact Confirm with By category and Not spending folded. Confirm, Undo and Was X → Save are kept.

Trade-off: Two more messages in the chat; §4.9’s “one review card” wording changes.

1b · One card, folded. Keeps one card: the total, Two to check open with Keep and Change, the rest folded.

Trade-off: A member can Confirm past the questions; still taller than a screen with them open.

1c · A short card, the review on a sheet. A one-line card with Review; three steps on a partial sheet (a dialog on the web).

Trade-off: A modal step outside the conversation; the sheet hides the chat.

c

Laptop widths 1133 to 1392 px: three panes squeeze the page

P2-3. With the 248 px sidebar and the 356 px chat, the page falls to 529 to 788 px and splits into two narrow columns from 700 px.

1a · The page decides its columns; the chat folds. One column until the page itself is 760 px; the chat stays beside from 1133 px with a fold button to a 64 px rail, remembered per member.

Trade-off: Between 1133 and 1363 px with the chat open the page is one long column.

1b · The sidebar folds to a rail. The sidebar becomes a 76 px icon rail below 1393 px.

Trade-off: Icons without words for twelve sections; the web’s navigation changes with the window, the iPad’s does not.

1c · The chat beside only from 1393 px. Below 1393 px the chat is a drawer from a Chat button.

Trade-off: Changes Arun’s width rule (MA-240) for the web only; an owner decision for every record.

d

Cards inside cards and long all-caps overlines

P2-2. About 340 boxes inside boxes and 53 overlines over 30 characters, drawn here on iPhone Home and the web’s Home with the same figures.

1a · Hairlines, sentence-case labels. One surface per card; rows and stats split by 1 px hairlines; the pulse sits on the hero; short sentence-case labels. No new token.

Trade-off: Dense screens (the Admin center) need sub-headings instead of boxes.

1b · Grouped lists, headers outside. Below the hero, iOS grouped inset lists with section names above them.

Trade-off: Reads as a settings page on the web; Monzo’s lifted warmth goes flat.

1c · Open sections, no cards. Only the hero stays a card; sections sit on the page between hairlines.

Trade-off: Weaker at 25% brightness in dark; a large change from Warm ledger as picked.

e

/join/<token> in a browser

P2-5. The invite page assumes the app: “open in the MyPaisa app” copy and dimmed Google and passkey buttons for someone who only uses a browser.

1a · One page, two live buttons. Who invited you, to which household, what a member can do; Continue with Apple and Continue with Google; a passkey offered right after joining; the used link drawn too.

Trade-off: None of note; the expired link reuses the used-link layout.

1b · Three short steps. The invite, then how you sign in, then the passkey offer, with a progress bar.

Trade-off: One more tap for everyone; a Continue that only turns the page.

1c · The invite is the chat’s first message. The join page is the web chat; the invite card carries the sign-in buttons.

Trade-off: A chat that cannot be typed into before sign-in looks broken.

f

The type and icon stack

P2-4. tokens.css loads Inter, Nunito and Material Symbols from Google Fonts through a render-blocking @import, and every icon, the app’s too, is a Material ligature.

1a · Platform type, Lucide on the web. SF Pro, SF Pro Rounded and SF Symbols in the app; on the web the system font, then self-hosted Inter and a small Nunito figures subset, with Lucide SVG icons.

Trade-off: The web differs slightly on Windows and Android (Inter) from Apple (SF Pro).

1b · Inter everywhere. One face at both doors, bundled in the app, square hero figures; icons as 1a.

Trade-off: The app stops looking native; the rounded hero figures picked in MA-101 go.

1c · Today’s look, self-hosted. Keep Nunito and Material Symbols, self-hosted, at both doors.

Trade-off: A ligature font still draws words when it fails; Material in the app goes against Apple’s guidelines.

g

⌘K on the web desktop

P1-7. TECH_STACK names a command palette, but no board draws it.

1a · One palette: go, find, or ask. ⌘K opens one palette: pages, chat flows and settings when empty; typed, “Ask MyPaisa: …” first, then records and flows. Nothing is created in it.

Trade-off: iPad’s ⌘K focuses the composer today; recorded as an open question.

1b · ⌘K is the chat. As on iPad, ⌘K focuses the chat beside the page, which suggests places as you type.

Trade-off: Below 1133 px the chat must open first; no palette for settings.

1c · Search only. ⌘K searches records and pages; questions stay in the chat on ⌘J.

Trade-off: Two keys; ⌘K means something else than on iPad.
