MA-241 Kind icons: measure every tile pair of every option, in light and dark, at full brightness and at 25%.

    python3 check.py            # read the colours back from the written board, measure, write contrast.txt; exit 1 on a fail

The model is scripts/contrast_check.py's: ratio (L1 + 0.05) / (L2 + 0.05) at full brightness, and
(0.25 L1 + 0.05) / (0.25 L2 + 0.05) with the screen at 25%. Tiers:
  ui        a glyph or a monogram on its tile (it carries the kind): 3:1, and 1.8:1 at 25%
  presence  a tile's fill on the card and on the page (it must be seen): 1.5:1, and 1.25:1 at 25%
A gradient tile (option B) is measured at both its stops, so the worst stop counts.
