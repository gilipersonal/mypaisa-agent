## Limits

The assessment does not cover every possible viewport, every frame or every transition. It does not establish production conformance, actual 25% screen-brightness behavior, native VoiceOver, Dynamic Type implementation, system gestures, touch hit-testing or hardware performance. Architecture pages, diagrams and historical design rounds were not reviewed as current product UX. The findings name their demonstrated scope.

The original values in these screenshots are illustrative design data, as documented by the source boards. The design files and their selected treatment were not altered to make the captures.

JSON field note: `frames` contains the enumerated device/browser elements (848 total). `count` is the capture-selector count, which falls back to labelled sections in six system/prototype files (938 in aggregate). It is not a second frame total and should not be presented as one.
